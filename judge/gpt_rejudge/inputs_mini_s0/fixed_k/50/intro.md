## Related Works

The proliferation of open-source machine learning (ML) models and datasets has catalyzed advances in information retrieval (IR) and resource management within the AI community. Hugging Face (HF) has emerged as a central platform hosting hundreds of thousands of models and datasets, fostering collaboration and reuse in diverse ML domains \[[2502.04484](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04484)\]\[[2311.1338](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13380)\]\[[2405.13058](http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13058)\]. However, despite HF’s popularity and extensive repository, existing studies reveal significant limitations in the structured representation and documentation of these resources, constraining advanced IR capabilities such as tracking model evolution, dataset recommendation, and comprehensive resource analysis.

Several large-scale empirical analyses have characterized the current state of ML resource documentation and supply chains on HF. For example, extensive documentation audits show substantial heterogeneity in dataset card completeness and quality, with key sections like usage considerations frequently under-documented, impacting reproducibility and transparency \[[2401.13822](http://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13822)\]. Similarly, model maintenance and evolution studies highlight the dynamics of model updates and community engagement but note challenges in consistent metadata and traceability across development stages \[[2311.1338](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13380)\]. These findings underscore the need for more structured, standardized, and accessible metadata to enable sophisticated resource management.

Efforts to explicitly structure ML knowledge have increasingly turned to knowledge graphs (KGs) due to their ability to encode entities, relationships, and rich attributes conducive to complex querying and reasoning. Prior works in scientific and medical domains illustrate the utility of KGs constructed via natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning techniques to represent entities and their interactions, facilitating better insight extraction and decision support \[[2307.04461](http://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04461)\]\[[2011.01103](http://arxiv.org/abs/2011.01103)\]. In the ML context, knowledge graphs have been leveraged to integrate heterogeneous information about models, datasets, and tasks, thereby improving resource discoverability and recommendation systems.

The limitations of existing platforms in offering structured, semantically rich representations have motivated the development of frameworks that combine KG construction with benchmarking. Benchmarks like HuggingBench, which integrate multi-task test collections for IR-related tasks on ML resources, exemplify the advancement toward systematic evaluation in this space. Benchmarking efforts elsewhere emphasize the importance of rigorous evaluation protocols for model performance and resource utility, which are critical for trust and reproducibility in ML research \[[2310.17644](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17644)\]. Notably, the integration of KGs with benchmark datasets supports nuanced analyses such as classification, recommendation, and tracing of ML resources, enabling researchers to move beyond flat metadata and unstructured descriptions.

Furthermore, the community dynamics and collaboration patterns on HF reveal a core-periphery structure with highly active developers at the core and many isolated contributors \[[2405.13058](http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13058)\]. This social structure affects resource dissemination and reuse, suggesting that knowledge graphs capturing developer relationships alongside resource metadata could enhance understanding and management of the open ML ecosystem. In addition, sustainability concerns such as carbon footprint reporting in ML models have been studied on HF, showing the potential for KG-based approaches to incorporate such metadata for responsible AI development \[[2305.11164](http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11164)\].

Other complementary research explores interoperability and tooling that leverage HF data, such as GAIA Search, which integrates IR techniques with HF datasets to facilitate large-scale textual corpus exploration \[[2306.01481](http://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01481)\]. These efforts demonstrate the feasibility and benefits of combining structured knowledge and retrieval systems to support the ML research community effectively.

In summary, the literature reveals a growing recognition of the importance of structured knowledge representation, standardized documentation, and comprehensive benchmarking for ML resources. The HuggingKG initiative aligns with this trajectory by constructing a large-scale knowledge graph from HF resources, enabling advanced IR tasks such as resource recommendation, classification, and tracing. This work builds on and extends prior empirical analyses of HF’s supply chain, documentation practices, community structure, and sustainability reporting, addressing existing gaps to enhance ML resource management and research reproducibility \[[2502.04484](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04484)\]\[[2401.13822](http://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13822)\]\[[2311.1338](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13380)\]\[[2305.11164](http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11164)\]\[[2405.13058](http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13058)\]\[[2310.17644](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17644)\]\[[2306.01481](http://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01481)\]\[[2307.04461](http://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04461)\]\[[2011.01103](http://arxiv.org/abs/2011.01103)\].
