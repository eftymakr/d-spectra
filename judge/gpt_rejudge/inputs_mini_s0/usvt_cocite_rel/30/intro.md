## Related Works

The problem of testing transaction isolation in databases has attracted significant attention, especially given the tension between strong isolation guarantees and system performance or availability. Classical results established that testing strong isolation levels such as serializability is NP-complete \[[1908.04509](http://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04509)\]\[[1806.08416](http://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08416)\]. This high complexity has motivated the exploration of weaker isolation levels, which relax consistency guarantees to improve throughput and availability, particularly in distributed and geo-replicated systems \[[1302.0309](http://arxiv.org/abs/1302.0309)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/615ff2896b52bee2cbfd64ced324106a29b73397](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/615ff2896b52bee2cbfd64ced324106a29b73397)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/32257d8d2b08c87e58c7b7f4b2430d58e4b51a81](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/32257d8d2b08c87e58c7b7f4b2430d58e4b51a81)\]. Such weak isolation levels include Read Committed (RC), Read Atomic (RA), Snapshot Isolation (SI), and Causal Consistency (CC).

Weak isolation levels are attractive because they enable higher concurrency and availability but introduce subtle anomalies that can compromise application correctness \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/405ae0ebf74cfbf5f2edec62e2966827dcdc9bef](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/405ae0ebf74cfbf5f2edec62e2966827dcdc9bef)\]\[[2003.10554](http://arxiv.org/abs/2003.10554)\]. Consequently, detecting isolation violations under these weaker models has become a critical research challenge. Recent work has demonstrated that testing certain weak isolation levels, such as RC and RA, is polynomial-time checkable, significantly improving the feasibility of testing large workloads compared to NP-complete cases like SI and serializability \[[1908.04509](http://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04509)\]\[[1905.08406](http://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08406)\]. Causal consistency can be tested even more efficiently, in linear time relative to the number of sessions \[[1908.04509](http://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04509)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cbe191bda20907994d45bc835d20446968370add](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cbe191bda20907994d45bc835d20446968370add)\].

Several black-box testing tools have been developed to detect isolation anomalies in production databases without relying on internal database details. For example, IsoVista provides a black-box isolation checker with guaranteed soundness and completeness for a range of isolation levels, coupled with visualization tools to aid debugging \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4f3081dbb68287f0d9d7bec3e7173e6c8ff035b9](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4f3081dbb68287f0d9d7bec3e7173e6c8ff035b9)\]. PolySI and Viper focus specifically on snapshot isolation, offering efficient, sound, and complete black-box checking algorithms that leverage novel graph-based representations of transaction dependencies and SMT solvers to detect SI violations \[[2301.07313](http://arxiv.org/abs/2301.07313)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f96a48d2bc0e77004b32960495685be6c301c63](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f96a48d2bc0e77004b32960495685be6c301c63)\]. PolySI has demonstrated the ability to discover new SI bugs in production cloud databases and outperform prior checkers, while Viper achieves significant speedups through heuristic pruning and supports SI variants \[[2301.07313](http://arxiv.org/abs/2301.07313)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f96a48d2bc0e77004b32960495685be6c301c63](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9f96a48d2bc0e77004b32960495685be6c301c63)\].

For a broader range of weak isolation levels, Plume represents a state-of-the-art system that combines modular anomalous patterns with vector and tree clock techniques to efficiently and completely check RC, RA, and CC levels in a black-box manner \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cbe191bda20907994d45bc835d20446968370add](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cbe191bda20907994d45bc835d20446968370add)\]. Plume reproduces known anomalies and discovers new bugs, outperforming existing approaches in both detection power and efficiency. These tools underscore the importance of efficient algorithms and scalable implementations for real-world testing of weak isolation.

Complementing these practical tools, theoretical advances have explored the complexity boundaries and algorithmic strategies for isolation testing. It has been shown that while serializability and SI testing are NP-complete in general, polynomial-time algorithms exist for RC, RA, and CC, often by exploiting structural properties of transaction histories and dependency graphs \[[1908.04509](http://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04509)\]\[[1905.08406](http://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08406)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/322ca3c1911230a84aab81b0f5ff8c2b543c56f7](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/322ca3c1911230a84aab81b0f5ff8c2b543c56f7)\]. Static analyses, including SMT-based techniques, have also been employed to verify application robustness against weak isolation levels, such as proving serializability under snapshot isolation by reducing the problem to state reachability in transactional programs \[[1905.08406](http://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08406)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/322ca3c1911230a84aab81b0f5ff8c2b543c56f7](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/322ca3c1911230a84aab81b0f5ff8c2b543c56f7)\]\[[1806.08416](http://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08416)\]. These approaches enable reasoning about correctness beyond runtime testing.

Testing under weak isolation is further complicated by the rarity and subtlety of anomalies in practical workloads. MonkeyDB, a mock storage system, addresses this by generating weak behaviors probabilistically to help developers identify assertion failures that only occur under weak isolation \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/615ff2896b52bee2cbfd64ced324106a29b73397](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/615ff2896b52bee2cbfd64ced324106a29b73397)\]. This complements black-box checkers by providing stress-testing capabilities for application robustness.

Overall, while existing testers have enabled significant progress, their polynomial-time complexity often remains high, limiting scalability to very large workloads typical in modern databases. The development of AWDIT, which achieves asymptotically optimal runtimes of \(O(n^{3/2})\) for RC and RA and \(O(n \cdot k)\) for CC, represents an important advance by substantially reducing computational overhead and enabling efficient testing of histories much larger than previously feasible. This line of work builds on and surpasses prior advances in both theoretical foundations and practical tooling for weak isolation verification \[[1908.04509](http://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04509)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cbe191bda20907994d45bc835d20446968370add](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/cbe191bda20907994d45bc835d20446968370add)\]\[[1905.08406](http://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08406)\].
