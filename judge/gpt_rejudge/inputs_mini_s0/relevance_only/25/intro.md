## Related Works

The integration of knowledge graphs (KGs) with large language models (LLMs) for claim verification and reasoning has garnered increasing attention as a means to overcome the limitations of LLMs in handling structured knowledge and multi-step reasoning. While LLMs excel in natural language understanding and generation, they often struggle with hallucinations, outdated or incomplete knowledge, and complex reasoning over structured data sources such as KGs \[[2305.13168](http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13168)\]\[[2410.20724](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20724)\]\[[2505.20099](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20099)\]. KGs, in contrast, provide explicitly structured, semantically rich factual information that can enhance reasoning fidelity and interpretability when effectively combined with LLMs.

Several works have explored frameworks that unify KGs and LLMs to improve knowledge extraction, reasoning, and question answering. For instance, approaches like AutoKG leverage multi-agent systems combining LLMs and external sources for KG construction and reasoning, highlighting LLMs' stronger aptitude as inference assistants rather than few-shot extractors \[[2305.13168](http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13168)\]. Similarly, frameworks such as QA-GNN perform joint reasoning over KG nodes and question-answering contexts by integrating graph neural networks with LLMs to enhance interpretability and handle complex reasoning tasks \[[2104.06378](http://arxiv.org/abs/2104.06378)\]. Other methods, including Logic-Query-of-Thoughts, break down complex logic queries into subquestions that combine KG reasoning with LLM inference, significantly improving accuracy on multi-step logical reasoning \[[2404.04264](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04264)\].

Addressing the challenge of hallucinations and outdated knowledge in LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) techniques have been applied to ground LLM responses in KG-derived subgraphs. SubgraphRAG introduces efficient subgraph retrieval via triple-scoring and structural distance encoding, balancing retrieval size and reasoning capacity to reduce hallucinations and improve response reliability across benchmarks \[[2410.20724](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20724)\]. Complementarily, the R2-KG framework employs a dual-agent system where a low-capacity LLM gathers evidence from the KG and a high-capacity LLM supervises final reasoning decisions, improving cost efficiency and trustworthiness by abstaining when evidence is insufficient \[[2502.12767](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12767)\].

Other research emphasizes faithful and interpretable reasoning by leveraging KG structural information beyond factual content. Methods like Reasoning on Graphs (RoG) generate KG-grounded relation paths as planning steps to retrieve valid reasoning paths, enabling LLMs to perform transparent and faithful reasoning, achieving state-of-the-art results on KGQA tasks \[[2310.01061](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01061)\]. Paths-over-Graph (PoG) further innovates by dynamically exploring multi-hop reasoning paths in KGs, pruning irrelevant information to ensure faithful, interpretable, and efficient reasoning in multi-entity question answering scenarios \[[2410.14211](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14211)\]. MindMap employs KG prompting to evoke LLM "mind maps," revealing reasoning pathways and blending implicit LLM knowledge with external KG facts, which enhances inference robustness and reduces hallucination \[[2308.09729](http://arxiv.org/abs/2308.09729)\].

In the specific domain of claim verification, surveys and frameworks illustrate the potential of integrating KGs with LLMs to improve fact-checking accuracy and reasoning depth. The survey on claim verification highlights the rise of LLM-based methods that utilize Retrieval-Augmented Generation and multi-step reasoning to verify claims against external evidence \[[2408.14317](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14317)\]. Reasoning-CV proposes a fine-tuning paradigm that generates Chain-of-Thought verification paths for complex claims without decomposing them into sub-claims, enhancing verification performance and mitigating decomposition errors \[[2505.12348](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12348)\]. ClaimsKG, a KG of fact-checked claims, supports structured querying of claim veracity and metadata, enabling advanced fact-checking applications reliant on KGs \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/08cc15080d093fa776e9b3377bc96a67ea2693e4](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/08cc15080d093fa776e9b3377bc96a67ea2693e4)\].

Moreover, the unification roadmap for LLMs and KGs categorizes integration approaches into KG-enhanced LLMs, LLM-augmented KGs, and synergistic frameworks where LLMs and KGs mutually reinforce each other for bidirectional reasoning. This taxonomy underlines the complementary strengths of both models and sets research directions for leveraging them in claim verification and other knowledge-intensive tasks \[[2306.08302](http://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08302)\].

Challenges remain in scaling these systems to handle diverse and evolving KGs, maintaining efficiency, and ensuring generalizability across datasets. For instance, KG-LLM-Bench provides a benchmark to evaluate the impact of different KG textualization strategies on LLM reasoning, facilitating systematic performance comparison \[[2504.07087](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07087)\]. KGLens offers an efficient probing method to measure alignment between LLM outputs and KG facts, aiding in identifying LLM hallucination and blind spots relevant for trustworthy claim verification \[[2312.11539](http://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11539)\]. Visualization tools like LinkQ assist users in constructing KG queries via LLMs and evaluating output accuracy, although they also reveal risks of user overtrust when LLMs err \[[2406.06621](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06621)\]\[[2505.21512](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21512)\].

In summary, existing works demonstrate that combining LLMs with KGs improves reasoning accuracy, interpretability, and trustworthiness for claim verification. However, most approaches face limitations in handling modular multi-step pipelines and zero-shot generalization to unstructured data, challenges that ClaimPKG aims to address by employing pseudo-subgraph representations and dedicated subgraph retrieval to tightly integrate KG knowledge with LLM reasoning \[[2305.13168](http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13168)\]\[[2410.20724](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20724)\]\[[2310.01061](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01061)\]\[[2502.12767](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12767)\]\[[2306.08302](http://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08302)\]\[[2404.04264](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04264)\]\[[2410.14211](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14211)\]\[[2505.12348](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12348)\].
