## Related Works

The rapid expansion of open-source machine learning (ML) resources, particularly on platforms like Hugging Face, has spurred significant research into resource management, model reuse, documentation, and the evolution of ML artifacts. However, despite this growth, the structured representation of these resources remains limited, constraining advanced retrieval and analytical tasks such as tracing model evolution and recommending relevant datasets.

Extensive empirical analyses have been conducted on Hugging Face to understand the ecosystem of ML models and datasets. For instance, large-scale studies have characterized the documentation quality and completeness of datasets, revealing heterogeneity correlated with popularity and a general lack of comprehensive usage considerations, which impedes reproducibility and accessibility \[[2401.13822](http://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13822)\]. Similarly, investigations into model maintenance and evolution have identified iterative development patterns aligned with data science methodologies, highlighting the role of commits and releases in refining model outputs and documentation \[[2311.13380](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13380)\]\[[2411.09645](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09645)\]. These findings underscore the dynamic and collaborative nature of ML model development on Hugging Face, yet they also point to the challenge of tracking these changes systematically.

Research has also highlighted the challenges and benefits of pre-trained model (PTM) reuse within the Hugging Face community. Key issues include missing provenance information, discrepancies between claimed and actual performance, and security risks \[[2303.02552](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.02552)\]\[[2401.13177](http://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13177)\]. Documentation quality has been strongly correlated with model popularity and reuse, emphasizing the need for better metadata and structured descriptions to support user decision-making \[[2406.08205](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08205)\]\[[2401.13177](http://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13177)\]. Furthermore, studies have emphasized the necessity of standardized licensing and improved license management practices to mitigate compliance risks in ML supply chains \[[2502.04484](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04484)\]\[[2405.13058](http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13058)\].

The security dimension of model sharing has gained attention, particularly concerning the use of unsafe serialization methods that expose models to supply chain attacks. Analyses have demonstrated the pervasiveness of vulnerabilities in Hugging Face repositories and proposed automated detection techniques to enhance platform safety \[[2410.04490](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04490)\]. This highlights the importance of integrating security considerations into ML resource management frameworks.

Environmental concerns have also been studied, with repository mining revealing the state of carbon footprint reporting for ML models on Hugging Face. Findings indicate a stalled adoption of carbon emission disclosures despite the increasing size and complexity of models, underscoring the need for standardized reporting and classification schemes to promote sustainable model development \[[2305.11164](http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11164)\].

On the tooling and infrastructure side, frameworks such as torchdistill have been integrated with Hugging Face libraries to facilitate reproducible deep learning experiments without extensive coding, showcasing the synergy between modular frameworks and large model repositories \[[2310.17644](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17644)\]. Moreover, metadata integration efforts, including the construction of AI pipeline knowledge graphs, demonstrate the potential of semantic enrichment and unified ontologies to enable better search, recommendation, and optimization of AI resources \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/05eb2cedfbfabfd035dc9a2b6c300f754f140d1d](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/05eb2cedfbfabfd035dc9a2b6c300f754f140d1d)\]. These approaches align with the goals of structured knowledge graphs like HuggingKG to provide comprehensive and navigable representations of ML resources.

Community and social aspects have been quantitatively analyzed, revealing highly skewed activity distributions where a small core of developers and organizations drive most contributions and usage \[[2405.13058](http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13058)\]\[[2503.17000](http://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17000)\]. Gender diversity studies expose significant underrepresentation of women in Hugging Face organizations, pointing to broader inclusion challenges in open-source AI ecosystems \[[2503.17000](http://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17000)\]. Understanding these collaborative dynamics is critical for designing inclusive and sustainable resource management platforms.

Finally, the integration of large language models (LLMs) as central orchestrators to connect diverse AI models within platforms like Hugging Face has been proposed to tackle complex multi-domain tasks, highlighting the evolving role of ML resource hubs beyond mere repositories to active, composable AI ecosystems \[[2303.17580](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17580)\].

In summary, prior work has established a rich empirical foundation on the documentation, evolution, reuse, security, sustainability, and community dynamics of Hugging Face resources. These studies collectively motivate the development of structured, large-scale knowledge graphs and benchmarking suites that can more effectively support IR tasks such as resource recommendation, classification, and tracing model evolution, as targeted by HuggingKG and HuggingBench.
