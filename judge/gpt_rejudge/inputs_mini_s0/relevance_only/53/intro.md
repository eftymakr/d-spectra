## Related Works

Sequential recommendation has witnessed significant advancements through the adoption of attention mechanisms and deep learning architectures aimed at capturing user behavior dynamics. Earlier approaches such as Markov Chains (MCs) and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) provided foundations to model sequential dependencies, with MCs excelling in sparse datasets and RNNs better handling denser ones by capturing longer-term semantics \[[1808.09781](http://arxiv.org/abs/1808.09781)\]. The emergence of self-attention mechanisms, notably the SASRec model, improved efficiency and effectiveness by adaptively focusing on relevant past items, bridging the gap between MC and RNN capabilities \[[1808.09781](http://arxiv.org/abs/1808.09781)\].

Despite these improvements, conventional attention-based models often rely on simple positional embeddings that fail to leverage rich temporal information inherent in user interactions. MEANTIME addresses this limitation by introducing multiple types of temporal embeddings to capture diverse temporal patterns and employing a mixture of attention mechanisms to exploit this diversity more fully \[[2008.08273](http://arxiv.org/abs/2008.08273)\]. Similarly, TiSASRec explicitly incorporates time intervals between interactions into the self-attention framework, enhancing the modeling of temporal dynamics beyond simple order information \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9c7a455b1e48d01a99e58e884a6c1acb75074ad0](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9c7a455b1e48d01a99e58e884a6c1acb75074ad0)\]. TLSRec further advances this line of work by hierarchically modeling user preferences at global and local time scales and adaptively fusing long-term and short-term preferences based on time lags, allowing finer-grained temporal sensitivity \[[2208.04760](http://arxiv.org/abs/2208.04760)\]. TLSTSRec also contributes by utilizing trainable timestamp matrices and adaptive fusion layers to integrate long- and short-term temporal information, addressing variability in user preferences over time \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/417f89b6992377e3e5d706672bfa4da837f011df](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/417f89b6992377e3e5d706672bfa4da837f011df)\].

State-space models (SSMs) have gained attention for their ability to capture continuous temporal dynamics with linear computational complexity, making them particularly suitable for handling variable-length sequences efficiently. SS4Rec introduces a hybrid SSM-based framework that models both temporal evolution and contextual dependencies, thereby overcoming the discrete-time constraints of previous RNN and attention models \[[2502.08132](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08132)\]. This aligns with the proposed STAR-Rec’s use of state-space modeling to compress temporal dynamics while maintaining efficiency in processing sequences of varying lengths.

Modeling diverse user behaviors remains a critical challenge in sequential recommendation. Mixture-of-experts (MoE) architectures have been proposed to address this by adaptively routing inputs to specialized expert modules. LimaRec introduces an incremental multi-interest self-attention model that captures diverse user interests and supports online updates, demonstrating the effectiveness of MoE in lifelong sequential modeling \[[2105.14060](http://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14060)\]. Other MoE frameworks have shown success in multi-modal and multi-task recommendation contexts, dynamically adapting to different behavioral patterns and improving recommendation quality \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/eb2ad6471c9e665a6393383a2deb2224f1ef07d7](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/eb2ad6471c9e665a6393383a2deb2224f1ef07d7)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0b42168f22713c48d8b0ce3bc8f680d347184d88](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0b42168f22713c48d8b0ce3bc8f680d347184d88)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ca9ca2e2f37e76de777db5204582b60cbb71637](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ca9ca2e2f37e76de777db5204582b60cbb71637)\]. This adaptive routing concept is central to STAR-Rec, which leverages a sequence-level MoE to handle both focused category-specific browsing and diverse exploratory behaviors.

Beyond purely sequential modeling, integrating side information and contextual signals has been recognized as beneficial. KATRec incorporates knowledge graphs to enrich item representations and better capture both short- and long-term user interests \[[2012.03323](http://arxiv.org/abs/2012.03323)\]. Similarly, models integrating spatiotemporal information and user portrait features have shown improvements in recommendation accuracy by capturing richer contextual dynamics \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ca9ca2e2f37e76de777db5204582b60cbb71637](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ca9ca2e2f37e76de777db5204582b60cbb71637)\]. Self-supervised learning approaches like S3-Rec utilize auxiliary objectives to improve data representations and robustness against data sparsity \[[2008.07873](http://arxiv.org/abs/2008.07873)\]. CauseRec enhances robustness further by synthesizing counterfactual user sequences to learn stable user representations less sensitive to noise \[[2109.05261](http://arxiv.org/abs/2109.05261)\].

Theoretical perspectives on attention mechanisms have also evolved. Probabilistic Attention reframes attention as dependency interactions modeled via Determinantal Point Processes (DPPs), emphasizing both relevance and diversity in recommendations—an aspect crucial for handling diverse user preferences \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d0c549ff283f4881a52a0c5ebf89be79a2af2407](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d0c549ff283f4881a52a0c5ebf89be79a2af2407)\]. Universal sequence representation learning approaches, such as UniSRec, highlight the importance of transferable item and sequence embeddings across domains, which may complement mixture-of-experts strategies for generalizable recommendation \[[2206.05941](http://arxiv.org/abs/2206.05941)\].

In summary, the literature converges on three key themes relevant to STAR-Rec: (1) the integration of sophisticated temporal modeling through advanced attention mechanisms and state-space models to capture both local and global time dynamics efficiently \[[1808.09781](http://arxiv.org/abs/1808.09781)\]\[[2008.08273](http://arxiv.org/abs/2008.08273)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9c7a455b1e48d01a99e58e884a6c1acb75074ad0](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/9c7a455b1e48d01a99e58e884a6c1acb75074ad0)\]\[[2208.04760](http://arxiv.org/abs/2208.04760)\]\[[2502.08132](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08132)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/417f89b6992377e3e5d706672bfa4da837f011df](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/417f89b6992377e3e5d706672bfa4da837f011df)\], (2) the use of mixture-of-experts frameworks to adaptively model heterogeneous user behaviors and preferences \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/eb2ad6471c9e665a6393383a2deb2224f1ef07d7](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/eb2ad6471c9e665a6393383a2deb2224f1ef07d7)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0b42168f22713c48d8b0ce3bc8f680d347184d88](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0b42168f22713c48d8b0ce3bc8f680d347184d88)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ca9ca2e2f37e76de777db5204582b60cbb71637](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ca9ca2e2f37e76de777db5204582b60cbb71637)\]\[[2105.14060](http://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14060)\], and (3) the enrichment of sequential models with contextual, side information, and self-supervised learning to improve robustness and recommendation quality \[[2012.03323](http://arxiv.org/abs/2012.03323)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ca9ca2e2f37e76de777db5204582b60cbb71637](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2ca9ca2e2f37e76de777db5204582b60cbb71637)\]\[[2206.05941](http://arxiv.org/abs/2206.05941)\]\[[2008.07873](http://arxiv.org/abs/2008.07873)\]\[[2109.05261](http://arxiv.org/abs/2109.05261)\]. STAR-Rec builds upon these foundations by unifying preference-aware attention and continuous-time state-space modeling under a flexible mixture-of-experts architecture, addressing challenges of sequence length variability and diverse interaction patterns in recommendation scenarios.
