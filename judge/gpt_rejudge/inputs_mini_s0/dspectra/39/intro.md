## Related Works

De novo genome assembly reconstructs an unknown genome from short or long sequencing reads, a process that remains computationally intensive and memory-demanding due to the complexity of genomic data and the volume of sequencing output \[[1705.11147](http://arxiv.org/abs/1705.11147)\]. Traditional assembly approaches primarily rely on graph-based models such as de Bruijn graphs and overlap-layout-consensus (OLC) frameworks to represent and resolve sequence overlaps \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f8d1052e2c9d1cdd63093427ccc9169e848d777d](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f8d1052e2c9d1cdd63093427ccc9169e848d777d)\]\[[1008.2555](http://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2555)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6e319a023f932d6d2ff8897c2c91e56e872db8e](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6e319a023f932d6d2ff8897c2c91e56e872db8e)\]. These methods face significant challenges in scaling efficiently to large genomes because of high memory consumption, irregular data structures, and the presence of repeats and heterozygosity \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f8d1052e2c9d1cdd63093427ccc9169e848d777d](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f8d1052e2c9d1cdd63093427ccc9169e848d777d)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3266b66caeb3986cbe6a299620af43c10eb2dee3](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3266b66caeb3986cbe6a299620af43c10eb2dee3)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d04b3cc774151260a59a5e6768b6cfae7fc3e512](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d04b3cc774151260a59a5e6768b6cfae7fc3e512)\].

Several distributed-memory parallel algorithms have been proposed to improve scalability and performance by exploiting high-performance computing resources. HipMER, for example, extends the Meraculous assembler with efficient parallelization strategies to assemble large genomes across thousands of cores, addressing computational bottlenecks in graph construction and traversal \[[1705.11147](http://arxiv.org/abs/1705.11147)\]. Similarly, diBELLA 2D employs distributed-memory sparse matrix algebra for overlap detection and layout simplification, achieving near-linear scaling and substantial speedups in transitive reduction for human and model organism genomes \[[2010.10055](http://arxiv.org/abs/2010.10055)\]. SWAP-Assembler introduces a Small World Asynchronous Parallel (SWAP) model that enables scalable graph cleaning and contig extension across thousands of cores, demonstrating improved N50 contig sizes and low error rates \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fe8d86bc5520b5049104ff58ee7e980982816ebf](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fe8d86bc5520b5049104ff58ee7e980982816ebf)\]. These distributed assemblers underscore the potential of parallelism but still contend with high memory footprints and irregular memory access patterns inherent to assembly graphs \[[1705.11147](http://arxiv.org/abs/1705.11147)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fe8d86bc5520b5049104ff58ee7e980982816ebf](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fe8d86bc5520b5049104ff58ee7e980982816ebf)\]\[[2010.10055](http://arxiv.org/abs/2010.10055)\].

Long-read sequencing technologies, such as PacBio SMRT and Oxford Nanopore, have enabled more contiguous assemblies by generating reads that span complex genomic regions. Assemblers like Canu leverage adaptive k-mer weighting and repeat separation to handle noisy long reads, achieving near-complete chromosome assemblies with graph-based outputs suitable for downstream phasing and scaffolding \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2b59fd18ada769ef76fb7fcc7382f063791abfab](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2b59fd18ada769ef76fb7fcc7382f063791abfab)\]. MHAP utilizes locality-sensitive hashing for probabilistic overlap detection of long reads, facilitating high-accuracy, near-complete eukaryotic assemblies with reduced computational overhead compared to traditional methods \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/618d82a202bb0408e388eba250c66c8a90700c81](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/618d82a202bb0408e388eba250c66c8a90700c81)\]. Minimap and miniasm provide ultra-fast mapping and assembly pipelines that bypass error correction, enabling rapid bacterial and eukaryotic genome assemblies, albeit with higher raw error rates in consensus sequences \[[1512.01801](http://arxiv.org/abs/1512.01801)\]. Despite improvements, long-read assembly workflows remain computationally demanding and require substantial memory resources \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/618d82a202bb0408e388eba250c66c8a90700c81](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/618d82a202bb0408e388eba250c66c8a90700c81)\]\[[1512.01801](http://arxiv.org/abs/1512.01801)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2b59fd18ada769ef76fb7fcc7382f063791abfab](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/2b59fd18ada769ef76fb7fcc7382f063791abfab)\].

Memory-efficient data structures have been developed to reduce the resource requirements of assembly. Succinct and compressed representations of de Bruijn graphs reduce storage needs by an order of magnitude compared to conventional structures, improving scalability on commodity hardware \[[1008.2555](http://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2555)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3db83bc91d0aa801301a9c063bbfd9d4202f89f2](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3db83bc91d0aa801301a9c063bbfd9d4202f89f2)\]. SOAPdenovo2 improves upon its predecessor by reducing memory consumption during graph construction and increasing coverage and contiguity, especially in repetitive regions, achieving significantly longer contig and scaffold N50s with lower memory usage \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f8d1052e2c9d1cdd63093427ccc9169e848d777d](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f8d1052e2c9d1cdd63093427ccc9169e848d777d)\]. Similarly, Cortex employs colored de Bruijn graphs to assemble multiple genomes simultaneously, enabling variant detection and genotyping without reliance on a reference genome \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91c964330beeed2c5c875d18633793a548a987cf](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/91c964330beeed2c5c875d18633793a548a987cf)\].

Assessing assembly quality remains a critical concern. Traditional metrics like N50 can be misleading, prompting the development of likelihood-based measures that estimate assembly quality de novo from the reads themselves, offering more objective and comprehensive evaluation without requiring a reference sequence \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d7827222e6d751e13b1fe74b97ce413a037abfaf](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/d7827222e6d751e13b1fe74b97ce413a037abfaf)\]. Assemblathon competitions have evaluated multiple assemblers across species, revealing variability in performance and highlighting that no single approach universally excels across all genomes \[[1301.5406](http://arxiv.org/abs/1301.5406)\].

Hardware acceleration and co-design approaches have started to address the computational bottlenecks in genome assembly. Although GPU implementations such as ELBA improve speed, they are often limited by memory bandwidth and data transfer overheads, and are less effective for irregular graph-based computations \[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7279acd9c61a8de019ea2d55d23124dfbb6b5269](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/7279acd9c61a8de019ea2d55d23124dfbb6b5269)\]. Recent efforts explore near-memory processing (NMP) architectures to alleviate memory bottlenecks by integrating processing elements closely with memory channels, enabling efficient handling of dynamic and interdependent data structures common in assembly graphs \[[2207.04350](http://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04350)\]. Quantum and quantum-inspired annealing methods have been experimentally applied to small genome assembly problems, showing potential for future acceleration but currently facing scalability limitations \[[2004.06719](http://arxiv.org/abs/2004.06719)\].

In summary, while distributed-memory assemblers and memory-efficient algorithms have advanced the scalability and accuracy of de novo genome assembly, challenges remain in managing large memory footprints, irregular data patterns, and computational overhead. Hardware-software co-designs, particularly those leveraging near-memory processing, represent a promising direction to overcome these limitations by reducing memory bottlenecks and increasing throughput for extreme-scale genome assembly \[[1705.11147](http://arxiv.org/abs/1705.11147)\]\[[https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fe8d86bc5520b5049104ff58ee7e980982816ebf](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fe8d86bc5520b5049104ff58ee7e980982816ebf)\]\[[2010.10055](http://arxiv.org/abs/2010.10055)\]\[[2207.04350](http://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04350)\].
