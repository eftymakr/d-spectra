## Related Works

The growing adoption of cloud-native applications has led to increased interest in optimizing resource management and cost efficiency in containerized environments. Prior research has primarily focused on system-level optimization strategies for cost and resource efficiency in containerized systems \[[1]()\]. However, the analysis of network costs in Kubernetes clusters remains underexplored, despite its critical role in overall cloud expenditures \[[2]()\]. This gap is addressed in this paper, which investigates the network usage and cost implications of containerized applications running on Kubernetes clusters.

Several studies have explored the use of Kubernetes for adaptive and cost-efficient resource management . These works highlight the importance of dynamic resource allocation to match workload demands while minimizing operational costs. However, they often overlook the specific challenges associated with network costs, which can significantly impact the financial viability of cloud-native applications . 

In parallel, researchers have proposed hierarchical multi-agent optimization frameworks for resource allocation in cloud computing . These approaches aim to improve resource utilization and reduce costs by enabling decentralized decision-making across distributed systems. While promising, such methods typically focus on computational resources rather than network-related expenditures, which are increasingly becoming a major cost factor in cloud environments .

The emerging discipline of FinOps, which combines financial management and cloud operations, has also gained traction in recent years . This field seeks to provide actionable insights for optimizing costs in cloud-based applications by integrating financial metrics with operational data. While some studies have begun to explore the financial implications of cloud resource usage , there is a lack of focused research on network cost analysis within Kubernetes clusters, which is a key component of FinOps practices .

Recent work has also emphasized the importance of cost modeling in cloud environments . These models help organizations predict and manage expenses by simulating various scenarios and evaluating trade-offs between performance and cost. However, most existing models do not account for the complexities of network costs in Kubernetes, which can vary based on factors such as data transfer rates, latency, and service provider pricing structures .

In summary, while existing research has made significant contributions to the fields of Kubernetes resource management and cloud cost optimization, the specific analysis of network costs in Kubernetes clusters remains a critical area for further investigation. This paper addresses this gap by providing a comprehensive analysis of network usage and cost implications in containerized applications running on Kubernetes clusters.
