## Related Works

Anomaly detection has been a focal point in machine learning, especially with the rise of intelligent manufacturing and the need for robust quality control in production lines \[[1' 2023-07-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06052v1)\]. Traditional approaches often rely on statistical models or supervised learning techniques, but these methods struggle with the complexity and variability of real-world industrial data \[[1' 2023-07-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06052v1)\]. Recent advances have focused on improving both the efficiency and effectiveness of anomaly detection models, particularly in resource-constrained environments \[[1' 2023-07-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06052v1)\].

Several studies have explored the use of deep learning for anomaly detection, particularly in image-based settings. The MVTec-AD dataset has been widely used to benchmark image anomaly detection models, with many approaches leveraging convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to extract features and detect anomalies \[[1' 2023-07-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06052v1)\]. One notable method is the PaDiM approach, which fits a multivariate Gaussian distribution to feature vectors and uses Mahalanobis distance as an anomaly score \[[1' 2023-07-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06052v1)\]. This method has been extended with a whitening transformation to enable visualization of anomalies, providing insights into model behavior \[[1' 2023-07-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06052v1)\].

For time series data, methods based on maximizing the Kullback-Leibler divergence between intervals have shown promise in detecting anomalies in multivariate time series \[[2' 2016-10-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06761v1)\]. These methods optimize over all possible intervals, improving detection accuracy compared to approaches that treat time steps independently \[[2' 2016-10-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06761v1)\]. Similarly, recurrent neural networks (RNNs) have been extensively studied for real-time lightweight anomaly detection, with evaluations across different deep learning frameworks highlighting the importance of selecting appropriate RNN variants and frameworks \[[7' 2024-07-26](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18439v1)\].

In the context of 3D objects and multi-view images, methods such as Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) have been proposed, but they often suffer from high computational costs \[[4' 2024-04-10](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06832v1)\]. To address this, the SplatPose framework has been introduced, which uses a 3D Gaussian splatting approach to estimate object poses and detect anomalies efficiently \[[4' 2024-04-10](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06832v1)\]. This approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in both training and inference speed, making it suitable for real-world deployment \[[4' 2024-04-10](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06832v1)\].

Another emerging area is the use of large language models (LLMs) for tabular anomaly detection. Pre-trained LLMs have demonstrated zero-shot batch-level anomaly detection capabilities, identifying low-density data regions without additional model fitting \[[5' 2024-06-24](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16308v1)\]. However, aligning LLMs to anomaly detection tasks requires fine-tuning strategies and synthetic datasets to improve performance \[[5' 2024-06-24](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16308v1)\].

In addition, graph neural networks (GNNs) have been applied to graph anomaly detection, with studies focusing on the spectral properties of graphs and the impact of anomalies on frequency distribution \[[11' 2022-05-31](http://arxiv.org/abs/2205.15508v1)\]. The Beta Wavelet Graph Neural Network (BWGNN) has been proposed to address the "right-shift" phenomenon, improving anomaly detection through spectral and spatial localized filters \[[11' 2022-05-31](http://arxiv.org/abs/2205.15508v1)\].

Finally, the importance of trustworthy anomaly detection has been emphasized, particularly in high-stakes applications such as fraud detection and cybersecurity \[[12' 2022-02-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07787v1)\]. Ensuring interpretability, fairness, and robustness in anomaly detection models is crucial for deploying these systems in real-world settings \[[12' 2022-02-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07787v1)\].
