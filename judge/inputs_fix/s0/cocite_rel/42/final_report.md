This paper contributes to the emerging field of FinOps by addressing the financial and operational aspects of managing network costs in cloud-native environments \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14753)\].

Prior research has focused on system-level optimizations for cost and resource efficiency in containerized systems, such as the use of Infrastructure-as-Code to alert engineering teams of expected deployment costs \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14753)\].
