The category "Pre-Trained Model Reusability Challenges" highlights several key issues faced by users when reusing large-scale pre-trained models (PTMs). These challenges include the lack of guidance for beginner users, difficulties in understanding model outputs during training or inference, and insufficient model documentation, which hinders comprehension and selection \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04484)\]. Additionally, there are discrepancies between claimed and actual model performance, and risks associated with using PTMs that are not well understood \[[2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13822)\]. These issues are exacerbated by the lack of standardized practices and infrastructure for managing PTMs, which complicates their integration into software systems \[[2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13822)\]. The study also notes that while some models maintain high upload rates, there is a decline in related discussion topics, indicating a potential disconnect between model availability and user engagement \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04484)\]. Furthermore, the introduction of documentation tools has not led to an increase in the quantity or quality of model documentation, further complicating model reuse \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04484)\]. These findings underscore the need for improved practices and infrastructure to support the effective reuse of PTMs in both research and industry settings.
