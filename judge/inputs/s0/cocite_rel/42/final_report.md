Jobs with diverse resource requirements (e.g. memory requirements) arrive over time and must be served by a cluster of servers, each with a finite resource capacity. This limitation highlights the need for more flexible algorithms that can adapt to complex workloads without prior knowledge of traffic patterns or resource distributions.
