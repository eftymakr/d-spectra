## Related Works

Brain tumor segmentation from 3D magnetic resonance images (MRIs) has been a focal point in medical image analysis, with numerous deep learning approaches proposed to improve accuracy and efficiency. Early methods relied on traditional image processing techniques, but these were limited in their ability to capture complex spatial relationships within brain tumors \[[13](http://arxiv.org/abs/1709.00382)\]. The advent of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) marked a significant advancement, enabling more accurate and automated segmentation \[[14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2011.11052)\]. The U-Net architecture, introduced by Ronneberger et al., became a cornerstone in biomedical image segmentation due to its symmetric encoder-decoder structure, which effectively captures context and enables precise localization \[[15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2103.04430)\]. This architecture has been widely adopted and modified for brain tumor segmentation tasks, with various improvements addressing issues such as class imbalance and overfitting \[[16](http://arxiv.org/abs/1811.02629)\].

Several studies have focused on enhancing the performance of U-Net-based models for brain tumor segmentation. For instance, the use of data augmentation and loss functions such as the dice loss has been shown to improve segmentation accuracy \[[17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2110.02519)\]. Additionally, the integration of variational auto-encoders (VAEs) into segmentation networks has been proposed to regularize the decoder and improve the quality of the segmented regions \[[18](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/b9c3631120c171570b94f946273b98c32505fc9a)\]. The BraTS challenge has played a crucial role in benchmarking these methods, with various teams achieving notable results \[[19](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/f28e387d4229c5f690ce4570a391c0f47e7155c7)\]. For example, the method proposed in \[[20](http://arxiv.org/abs/2107.02314)\] achieved high Dice scores for whole tumor, tumor core, and enhancing tumor, demonstrating the effectiveness of deep learning approaches in this domain.

The challenge of training deep networks on limited datasets has led to the exploration of transfer learning and pre-trained models.
