## Related Works

Sensor calibration is a critical step in autonomous driving systems, enabling accurate data fusion from heterogeneous sensors such as radar, camera, and LiDAR. Prior work has focused on improving the robustness and accuracy of calibration methods, particularly in challenging environments where sensor data is sparse or noisy. For instance, \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0955326c67a947b5fac5fbae7238d04fc9adc251)\] presents a novel method for extrinsic calibration of a radar–LiDAR–camera system, addressing the sparse informativeness of radar measurements by introducing a special target design and a two-step optimization procedure. This approach leverages radar cross section estimates to improve the accuracy of vertical alignment. Similarly, \[[2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2103.07505)\] proposes a continuous-time approach for 3D radar-to-camera extrinsic calibration, which does not require specialized radar retroreflectors, making it more practical for real-world deployment.

The use of deep learning for sensor calibration has also gained traction. \[[3](http://arxiv.org/abs/1904.08743)\] introduces a data-driven method for automatic rotational radar-camera calibration without dedicated calibration targets, using a coarse and fine convolutional neural network. This approach is particularly useful in scenarios where dedicated calibration targets are not available. Additionally, \[[4](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/4d4dbbcf27d991aeab43dafb82a105bb5512b799)\] presents an open-source tool for extrinsic calibration of radar, camera, and LiDAR, enabling joint calibration of all three modalities. The tool is designed to handle real-world measurements and includes a study on how various factors of the calibration procedure affect the outcome.

In the context of online calibration, \[[5](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/fa7f4199640500d48c0671181ea6a8dce3141123)\] proposes an online calibration method based on moving object tracking, which is particularly useful for long-term reliability. This method leverages information from the detection and tracking pipeline to provide a resource-efficient solution. Furthermore, \[[6](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c80265fa719eb85c8a56a6b36c74b99b793f39b2)\] introduces an automatic targetless method for extrinsic calibration of multiple 3D LiDAR and radar sensors, requiring only a short driving distance. This method is effective in environments where calibration targets are not available.

Recent work has also focused on feature-level fusion of radar and camera data. \[[7](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/0e88fc24ae7694d0b1a2fe78322936fc568bc7d2)\] presents RVDet, a feature-level fusion detection framework that aligns radar and camera features in the bird's-eye view (BEV) domain.
