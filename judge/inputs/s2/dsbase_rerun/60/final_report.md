This limitation has led to a growing body of work on calibration techniques that aim to refine the absolute confidence scores of these models \[[2' 2019-08-14](http://arxiv.org/abs/1908.05164v3)\]. Calibration is essential for downstream tasks that require precise probability estimates, such as recommendation systems and business metrics optimization \[[3' 2022-08-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2208.06164v2)\].

Several approaches have been proposed to calibrate ranking models. One line of work involves the use of parametric calibration methods, such as Gaussian and Gamma calibration, which map the ranking scores of pre-trained models to well-calibrated preference probabilities \[[1' 2021-12-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2112.07428v2)\]. These methods are effective but often rely on fixed functional forms, such as piece-wise linear functions, which may limit their flexibility in complex scenarios \[[4' 2014-08-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/1408.2764v2)\]. In contrast, non-parametric approaches, such as temperature scaling, have also been explored, though they may not always achieve the desired level of calibration \[[5' 2023-03-19](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10761v1)\].

A more flexible approach is the use of neural networks to model the calibration function.
