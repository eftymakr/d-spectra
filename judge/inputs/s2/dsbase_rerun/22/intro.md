## Related Works

Unsupervised domain adaptation (UDA) for semantic segmentation (UDA-SS) has been an active research area due to its importance in real-world applications where labeled target data is scarce \[[1' 2021-11-24](http://arxiv.org/abs/2111.12358v2)\]. Traditional UDA-SS methods typically assume that the category settings between the source and target domains are known, which is a restrictive assumption in practical scenarios \[[2' 2018-04-23](http://arxiv.org/abs/1804.08286v1)\]. This assumption leads to performance degradation when private classes (classes present in the target domain but not in the source) exist \[[3' 2022-04-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02111v1)\]. Several works have attempted to address this limitation by introducing more flexible frameworks that can handle unknown category configurations \[[4' 2021-03-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2103.15467v2)\].

One early approach to UDA-SS is the Fully Convolutional Adaptation Networks (FCAN), which combines Appearance Adaptation Networks (AAN) and Representation Adaptation Networks (RAN) to adapt models from synthetic to real-world domains \[[5' 2023-02-13](http://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06378v1)\]. FCAN focuses on both visual appearance-level and representation-level domain adaptation, achieving state-of-the-art results on the GTA5-to-Cityscapes dataset \[[6' 2024-05-30](http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19899v1)\]. Similarly, the Birds of a Feather Flock Together (BOAFT) method introduces an Inter-class Separation and Intra-class Aggregation (ISIA) mechanism to improve category-level alignment and reduce class confusion \[[7' 2019-10-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/1910.13049v2)\]. This approach also incorporates an Adaptive-weighted Instance Matching (AIM) strategy to optimize instance-level adaptation \[[8' 2021-03-25](http://arxiv.org/abs/2103.13561v1)\].

Another notable work is the Category-Adaptive Domain Adaptation (CAD) method, which introduces adversarial learning to bridge the style gap between domains and a category-adaptive threshold mechanism to improve pseudo label quality \[[9' 2021-03-24](http://arxiv.org/abs/2103.13041v1)\]. This approach achieves significant performance gains on the GTA5-to-Cityscapes dataset \[[10' 2022-11-26](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14596v1)\]. Additionally, the SPCL framework introduces semantic prototype-based contrastive learning to improve pixel-level discriminability and reduce domain shift \[[11' 2022-08-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2208.06087v1)\]. It explicitly encourages intra-class pixel representations to be closer and inter-class representations to be further apart \[[12' 2020-12-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2012.08226v2)\].

The Open-Set Domain Adaptation for Semantic Segmentation (OSDA-SS) approach addresses the challenge of unknown classes in the target domain by introducing a Boundary and Unknown Shape-Aware (BUS) method \[[13' 2022-01-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2201.01002v1)\]. This method uses a novel dilation-erosion-based contrastive loss to accurately discern boundaries between known and unknown classes \[[14' 2021-09-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2109.14219v1)\]. Additionally, the Category Anchor-Guided UDA (CAG-UDA) method introduces category-wise centroids to guide feature alignment and improve classification accuracy \[[15' 2017-02-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/1702.05374v2)\].
