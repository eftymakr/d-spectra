## Related Works

Single-pixel imaging (SPI) has gained significant attention due to its ability to capture images outside the range of conventional focal plane array detectors \[[2' 2023-10-25](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16869v2)\]. However, iterative reconstruction methods used in SPI often suffer from limited image quality and lengthy computational times \[[2' 2023-10-25](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16869v2)\]. To address these challenges, deep learning has been introduced into SPI, offering exceptional reconstruction quality and fast reconstruction speeds \[[2' 2023-10-25](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16869v2)\]. These deep learning-based approaches have been applied in various domains, including single-pixel imaging through scattering media, photon-level imaging, and color single-pixel imaging \[[2' 2023-10-25](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16869v2)\].

Deep algorithm unrolling has emerged as a powerful model-based approach that combines the interpretability of iterative algorithms with the performance gains of supervised deep learning \[[4' 2021-09-28](http://arxiv.org/abs/2109.14025v1)\]. This framework is particularly useful in biological imaging, where physics-based models describe the measurement process and the information to be recovered is highly structured \[[4' 2021-09-28](http://arxiv.org/abs/2109.14025v1)\]. Unrolling methods were first proposed to develop fast neural network approximations for sparse coding, and they have since attracted significant attention due to their potential in creating efficient and interpretable network architectures \[[3' 2019-12-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/1912.10557v3)\].

In the context of image deconvolution, unrolling the Half-Quadratic Splitting (HQS) algorithm has been explored to retain algorithm properties while enhancing performance. A new parametrization scheme was introduced to enforce layer-specific parameters to asymptotically approach fixed points, leading to improved performance and convergence guarantees \[[5' 2024-02-20](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12872v2)\]. This approach was validated through extensive experiments and demonstrated convergence as the number of layers increases \[[5' 2024-02-20](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12872v2)\].

For image enhancement tasks, deep internal learning methods have been proposed to improve the perceptual quality of images without relying on extensive training data \[[6' 2020-12-11](http://arxiv.org/abs/2012.06469v1)\]. These methods focus on enhancing content and style features while preserving image context through contextual content loss \[[6' 2020-12-11](http://arxiv.org/abs/2012.06469v1)\]. The proposed framework outperformed state-of-the-art works in image enhancement tasks \[[6' 2020-12-11](http://arxiv.org/abs/2012.06469v1)\].

In the realm of trajectory prediction, deep learning models have been used to predict vessel trajectories by fusing forward and reverse sub-networks, leading to significant improvements in trajectory prediction accuracy \[[26' 2022-01-19](http://arxiv.org/abs/2201.07606v2)\]. These models leverage attention mechanisms and bidirectional LSTM to mine historical trajectory data effectively \[[26' 2022-01-19](http://arxiv.org/abs/2201.07606v2)\].

For medical imaging, deep learning has been applied to enhance image quality and improve diagnostic accuracy. For instance, diffusion probabilistic models have been used to augment datasets for breast cancer detection, significantly improving classification accuracy \[[22' 2024-07-01](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00967v1)\]. Additionally, deep learning has been applied to detect anomalies in vehicle trajectories, leveraging contrastive learning and trajectory semantics for effective anomaly detection \[[30' 2023-03-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05000v1)\].
