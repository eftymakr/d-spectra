## Related Works

Recent advancements in Text-to-Speech (TTS) systems have significantly enhanced speech naturalness, driving the need for precise prosody control and mispronunciation correction \[[1' 2023-03-02](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01086v1)\]. Traditional approaches for prosody manipulation often rely on specialized modules or additional training, limiting their capacity for post-hoc adjustments \[[1' 2023-03-02](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01086v1)\]. Similarly, conventional mispronunciation correction methods depend on grapheme-to-phoneme (G2P) dictionaries, which are impractical in low-resource settings \[[1' 2023-03-02](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01086v1)\]. This section reviews related works that address these challenges, focusing on G2P conversion, prosody modeling, and mispronunciation correction.

Grapheme-to-phoneme (G2P) conversion is a crucial step in TTS systems, mapping graphemes to phonetic representations \[[2' 2023-07-31](http://arxiv.org/abs/2307.16643v1)\]. Existing methods often rely on manually annotated dictionaries, which are time-consuming and costly to acquire \[[2' 2023-07-31](http://arxiv.org/abs/2307.16643v1)\]. Recent work has explored neural approaches, such as the Transformer-based G2P model, which outperforms recurrent and convolutional neural network-based approaches in terms of word error rate \[[13' 2020-04-14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2004.06338v2)\]. Additionally, LiteG2P, a fast and light model, demonstrates superior performance with fewer parameters and less computation compared to state-of-the-art methods \[[1' 2023-03-02](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01086v1)\]. For Chinese G2P conversion, the Good Neighbors approach leverages neighboring context to improve disambiguation of polyphones \[[11' 2023-03-14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.07726v1)\]. Similarly, the Reinforcer method enhances G2P performance by emphasizing phonological information between neighboring characters \[[11' 2023-03-14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.07726v1)\].

Prosody modeling is essential for natural speech synthesis, as it governs stress, intonation, and rhythm \[[12' 2024-10-31](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24019v1)\]. End-to-end TTS systems, such as Prosody-TTS, combine the advantages of statistical parametric models and neural networks, explicitly modeling phoneme duration and fundamental frequency (f0) for controllable synthesis \[[6' 2021-10-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2110.02854v1)\]. Unsupervised word-level prosody tagging has also been explored, enabling controllable speech synthesis without explicit prosody tags \[[7' 2022-02-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07200v2)\]. DiffStyleTTS further advances prosody modeling by hierarchically modeling speech prosodic features and controlling different prosodic styles \[[8' 2024-12-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03388v1)\].

Mispronunciation detection and correction remain critical challenges in TTS, particularly in multilingual settings \[[9' 2023-03-01](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.00171v1)\]. ### <a id='category-g2p-conversion-methods'></a>G2P Conversion Methods: 12 papers found.
Grapheme-to-phoneme (G2P) conversion is a critical component in Text-to-Speech (TTS) systems, enabling the transformation of written text into phonetic representations \[[1' 2023-03-02](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01086v1)\]. Traditional methods often rely on manually curated pronunciation dictionaries, which are labor-intensive and not scalable for low-resource languages \[[6' 2021-10-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2110.02854v1)\]. Recent approaches have explored neural network-based solutions, such as connectionist temporal classification (CTC) models and transformer architectures, which offer better performance and adaptability \[[8' 2024-12-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03388v1)\]. For instance, LiteG2P combines expert knowledge with CTC-based neural networks to achieve fast and efficient G2P conversion with reduced parameters and computational cost \[[1' 2023-03-02](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01086v1)\]. Similarly, the use of large language models (LLMs) has shown promise in contextual G2P conversion by leveraging in-context knowledge retrieval to improve disambiguation of polyphones \[[4' 2024-11-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07563v1)\]. Additionally, methods like ByT5, which use byte-level encoding, have demonstrated effectiveness in word-level G2P but face challenges in handling sentence-level or paragraph-level conversions due to exposure bias \[[3' 2023-08-16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08442v1)\]. To address this, loss-based sampling methods have been proposed to enhance performance in such scenarios \[[3' 2023-08-16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08442v1)\]. Furthermore, data-driven approaches that eliminate the need for expert lexicons have been introduced, using self-supervised learning to obtain phoneme representations directly from raw data \[[6' 2021-10-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2110.02854v1)\]. These advancements highlight the evolving landscape of G2P conversion, emphasizing the shift from rule-based systems to more flexible and scalable neural models \[[1' 2023-03-02](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01086v1)\]\[[3' 2023-08-16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08442v1)\]\[[4' 2024-11-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07563v1)\]\[[6' 2021-10-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2110.02854v1)\].

### <a id='category-prosody-control-in-tts'></a>Prosody Control in TTS: 7 papers found.

Additionally, a method for voice conversion using an ASR-TTS system has been proposed, where a prosody code is used to describe prosody information, facilitating the transfer of source prosody to converted speech \[[4' 2024-11-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07563v1)\]. These approaches highlight the ongoing efforts to improve the naturalness and flexibility of TTS systems through advanced prosody control mechanisms.

We introduce Counterfactual Activation Editing, a model-agnostic method that manipulates internal representations in a pre-trained TTS model to achieve post-hoc control of prosody and pronunciation. Experimental results show that our method effectively adjusts prosodic features and corrects mispronunciations while preserving synthesis quality. This opens the door to inference-time refinement of TTS outputs without retraining, bridging the gap between pre-trained TTS models and editable speech synthesis.
