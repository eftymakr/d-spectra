## Related Works

Dense retrievers have become a cornerstone of modern information retrieval systems, leveraging pre-trained language models (e.g., BERT, LLaMA) that are fine-tuned via contrastive learning to encode text into dense representations for efficient comparison \[[1' 2022-10-23](http://arxiv.org/abs/2210.12607v1)\]. Recent research has questioned the relative importance of fine-tuning versus pre-training in dense retrievers, with some arguing that retrieval knowledge is primarily acquired during pre-training, and that fine-tuning does not significantly alter this knowledge \[[2' 2021-08-13](http://arxiv.org/abs/2108.06279v2)\].
