## Related Works

The growing adoption of cloud-native applications has led to increased interest in optimizing resource management and cost efficiency in containerized environments. Prior research has explored system-level optimization strategies for cost and resource efficiency in containerized systems \[[1]()\]. However, the analysis of network costs in Kubernetes clusters remains underexplored \[[2]()\]. This paper contributes to the emerging FinOps discipline by addressing the financial and operational aspects of managing network costs in cloud-native environments .

Several studies have investigated the use of Kubernetes for adaptive and cost-efficient resource management . These works highlight the importance of dynamic resource allocation and workload scheduling to reduce operational costs. For instance, some researchers have proposed hierarchical multi-agent optimization frameworks for resource allocation in cloud computing . These approaches leverage distributed decision-making to improve resource utilization and reduce waste. While these methods are promising, they often focus on computational resources rather than network costs, which are a critical component of cloud infrastructure expenses .

In the context of FinOps, recent efforts have aimed to integrate financial management with cloud operations to optimize costs in cloud-based applications . This includes the development of cost models that account for both compute and network expenditures. However, most existing models do not specifically address the unique challenges of network cost optimization in Kubernetes clusters . The paper under review fills this gap by providing a detailed analysis of network usage and cost implications in containerized applications running on Kubernetes .

A number of studies have also explored the use of measurement analysis and experimentation to evaluate the performance and cost of cloud-native systems . These works emphasize the importance of empirical validation in understanding the real-world behavior of cloud platforms. The methodology proposed in this paper builds on these insights by combining measurement analysis, experimentation, and cost modelling to provide actionable insights into network cost optimization .

In summary, while prior research has made significant contributions to the fields of containerization, resource management, and FinOps, there remains a need for more focused studies on network cost optimization in Kubernetes clusters. This paper addresses this gap by providing a comprehensive analysis of network usage and cost implications in containerized applications, contributing to the ongoing development of the FinOps discipline .
