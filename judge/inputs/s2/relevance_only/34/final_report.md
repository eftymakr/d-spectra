Fund allocation in financial domains has attracted significant research interest, with a focus on improving the accuracy and robustness of allocation strategies under various constraints. Traditional approaches often rely on prediction-only or Predict-Then-Optimize (PTO) frameworks, which may suffer from goal mismatch and uncertainty in forecasting \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03165)\]. Recent works have attempted to address these challenges by integrating advanced time series forecasting models with optimization techniques. The integration of deep learning models with financial forecasting has also been explored. For example, the use of Transformers in time series forecasting has gained traction due to their ability to model long-term dependencies \[[18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14730)\]. Additionally, the introduction of risk-aware frameworks has become increasingly important, as financial applications often require balancing profitability with risk management \[[3](http://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04662)\].
