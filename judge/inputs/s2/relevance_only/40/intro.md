## Related Works

Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs) have emerged as a critical tool in privacy-preserving computing, enabling a prover to demonstrate the truth of a statement without revealing additional information \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.05890)\]. Despite their potential, the computational complexity of generating ZKPs has limited their adoption in real-world applications \[[2](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e149d93531d8c07cf43f6cae327a4795144d1304)\]. Recent efforts have focused on accelerating the key primitives of ZKP protocols, particularly on GPU and ASIC platforms \[[3](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12481)\]. For instance, GZKP leverages a GPU-accelerated approach to improve the performance of ZKP systems by optimizing memory access patterns and reducing external shuffling \[[4](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07063)\]. Similarly, ReZK introduces a reconfigurable accelerator that targets the bottlenecks of Number Theoretic Transform (NTT) and Multi-scalar Multiplication (MSM) operations, achieving significant speedups \[[5](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/6ee84674b7c426849c3da56cb278c08eb7a903cf)\]. 

The challenge of generating ZKPs efficiently has also been addressed through specialized hardware solutions. SZKP, for example, is the first ASIC to accelerate an entire proof on-chip, leveraging structured dataflows for both NTT and MSM operations \[[6](http://arxiv.org/abs/2206.13350)\]. This approach achieves speedups of over 400× compared to CPU implementations \[[6](http://arxiv.org/abs/2206.13350)\]. Similarly, PipeZK proposes a pipelined architecture with two subsystems to handle the intensive compute tasks of polynomial computations and elliptic curve operations, achieving a 10× speedup on standard cryptographic benchmarks \[[7](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09784)\]. These works highlight the importance of optimizing the core primitives of ZKP systems to enable practical deployment.

In addition to hardware acceleration, there has been growing interest in improving the efficiency of ZKP protocols themselves. For example, the development of zkFi aims to integrate ZKPs into multi-chain protocols while preserving consumer privacy and ensuring regulatory compliance \[[8](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8b12309fd3c5a1851ff8b6d9cfff018811d08d93)\]. This middleware solution abstracts the complexity of ZKP implementation, making it easier for developers to adopt without requiring deep expertise in zero-knowledge proofs \[[8](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/8b12309fd3c5a1851ff8b6d9cfff018811d08d93)\]. Similarly, zksk provides a Python-based library for defining and computing sigma protocols, enabling developers to construct ZKPs without learning a new language \[[9](http://arxiv.org/abs/2403.15676)\]. These tools demonstrate the increasing accessibility of ZKP technology to a broader audience.

The performance limitations of ZKP systems have also been studied in the context of blockchain rollups. A recent analysis of ZKP-based rollups on Ethereum identified key bottlenecks in the ZKP mechanism, emphasizing the need for more efficient implementations to enhance scalability \[[10](http://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18176)\].
