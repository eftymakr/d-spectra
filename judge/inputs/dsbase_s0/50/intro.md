## Related Works

The rapid growth of open-source machine learning (ML) resources, such as models and datasets, has significantly influenced information retrieval (IR) research \[[Jiaoyan Chen' 2021-12-18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10006v6)\]. However, existing platforms like Hugging Face have limitations in utilizing structured representations, which hinders advanced queries and analyses such as tracing model evolution and recommending relevant datasets \[[Junyi Chen' 2024-09-25](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16674v3)\]. This section reviews related works that address these challenges and contribute to the development of knowledge graphs and benchmarking frameworks for ML resources.

Several studies have explored the use of knowledge graphs for organizing and managing ML resources. For instance, \[[Danilo Dessì' 2020-10-28](http://arxiv.org/abs/2011.01103v1)\] presents a framework for knowledge graph access that integrates with the PyData machine learning software stack, highlighting the importance of structured representations in enhancing the usability and interoperability of ML resources. Similarly, \[[Himan Abdollahpouri' 2020-06-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15772v2)\] introduces a structured methods description for supervised machine learning validation in biology, which supports the organization of ML resources in a domain-specific manner. These works emphasize the value of knowledge graphs in capturing relationships and attributes that are critical for advanced querying and analysis.

The construction of knowledge graphs for ML resources has been explored in several studies. For example, \[[Yuting Feng' 2022-10-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01942v1)\] discusses the use of learning curves to assess model performance and guide resource allocation, which is relevant to the dynamic nature of ML resources. Another study, \[[Yong Zheng' 2017-07-27](http://arxiv.org/abs/1707.08913v1)\], reviews strategies for selecting informative data points from data streams, which is essential for managing the growing volume of ML resources. These studies highlight the need for adaptive and scalable methods to effectively represent and manage ML resources.

The integration of knowledge graphs with benchmarking frameworks for ML resources is another area of active research. \[[Robin Burke' 2017-07-28](http://arxiv.org/abs/1707.09258v1)\] evaluates the performance of different models on various datasets, emphasizing the importance of benchmarking in ensuring the reliability of ML systems. Additionally, \[[Maximilian P Niroomand' 2023-04-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02381v2)\] discusses the challenges of maintaining privacy while using ML models in sensitive domains, which is relevant to the ethical and practical considerations of ML resource management. These studies underscore the necessity of rigorous evaluation and benchmarking to ensure the effectiveness and reliability of ML systems.

The development of benchmarking frameworks for ML resources has also been addressed in several works. \[[Diego Granziol' 2019-06-03](http://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01101v1)\] provides a comprehensive evaluation of different models on diverse datasets, demonstrating the importance of benchmarking in advancing the field of ML resource management. Furthermore, \[[Jessica Zosa Forde' 2019-04-24](http://arxiv.org/abs/1904.10922v1)\] explores the use of learning curves to guide model selection and resource allocation, which is crucial for optimizing the performance of ML systems. These studies demonstrate the importance of benchmarking in improving the efficiency and accuracy of ML resource management.

In summary, the related works highlight the importance of structured representations, adaptive methods, and rigorous benchmarking in managing and evaluating ML resources. These contributions form the foundation for the development of HuggingKG and HuggingBench, which aim to address the limitations of existing platforms and advance research in open-source resource sharing and management.
