## Related Works

Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs) have emerged as a critical tool in privacy-preserving computing, enabling verifiers to confirm the truth of a statement without learning additional information \[[Shahzad Ahmad Butt' 2024-12-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12481v1)\]. Despite their potential, ZKPs have faced adoption challenges due to the computational complexity of the proving process \[[Shahzad Ahmad Butt' 2024-12-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12481v1)\]. Recent advancements have focused on accelerating the key primitives of ZKP protocols using hardware accelerators like GPUs and ASICs \[[Shahzad Ahmad Butt' 2024-12-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12481v1)\]. However, existing solutions often require trusted setups or generate larger proof sizes, limiting their applicability in scenarios with numerous verifiers or strict verification time constraints \[[Shahzad Ahmad Butt' 2024-12-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12481v1)\].

Several works have explored hardware acceleration for ZKP protocols. For instance, *SZKP* presents a scalable accelerator framework that leverages structured dataflows for Number Theoretic Transform (NTT) and Multi-scalar Multiplication (MSM) operations, achieving speedups of over 400× compared to CPU implementations \[[Alhad Daftardar' 2024-08-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.05890v1)\]. Similarly, *if-ZKP* introduces an FPGA-based acceleration for zero-knowledge proofs, focusing on optimizing MSM computations using modular arithmetic and Intel IP Libraries \[[Divya Mahajan' 2018-01-08](http://arxiv.org/abs/1801.06027v2)\]. These accelerators demonstrate significant performance improvements but often require custom hardware or specific implementation details \[[Divya Mahajan' 2018-01-08](http://arxiv.org/abs/1801.06027v2)\].

In the realm of ZKP protocols, *zkSNARKs* have gained traction due to their succinctness and efficiency \[[Anees Ahmed' 2024-11-10](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06350v1)\]. However, the computational overhead of generating proofs remains a bottleneck. *zkFi* addresses this by proposing a middleware solution that integrates ZKPs into multi-chain protocols, enabling privacy-preserving transactions across domains such as DeFi and e-commerce \[[Oleg Verbitsky' 2008-01-31](http://arxiv.org/abs/0801.4917v1)\]. This approach abstracts away the complexities of ZKP implementation, making it more accessible to developers \[[Oleg Verbitsky' 2008-01-31](http://arxiv.org/abs/0801.4917v1)\].

The *HyperPlonk* protocol, which this work accelerates, is notable for its support of one-time, universal setup and small proof sizes, making it suitable for publicly verifiable, consensus-based systems \[[Shahzad Ahmad Butt' 2024-12-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12481v1)\]. Prior efforts to accelerate ZKP protocols have often focused on individual primitives, such as *SumCheck* and *MSMs*, but have not addressed the full protocol \[[Shahzad Ahmad Butt' 2024-12-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12481v1)\]. *zkSpeed* represents a significant advancement by accelerating the entire protocol, including these critical components, using a full-chip architecture with 366.46 mm² and 2 TB/s bandwidth \[[Shahzad Ahmad Butt' 2024-12-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12481v1)\].

The acceleration of *SumCheck* and *MSMs* has been explored in various contexts. For example, *SZKP* optimizes NTT and MSM operations using structured dataflows, achieving substantial speedups \[[Alhad Daftardar' 2024-08-12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.05890v1)\]. Similarly, *if-ZKP* leverages parallelism in MSM computations and modular arithmetic for performance gains \[[Divya Mahajan' 2018-01-08](http://arxiv.org/abs/1801.06027v2)\]. These optimizations are crucial for improving the efficiency of ZKP protocols, especially in applications requiring high throughput \[[Divya Mahajan' 2018-01-08](http://arxiv.org/abs/1801.06027v2)\].

In addition to hardware acceleration, *zkDL* introduces an efficient zero-knowledge proof for deep learning training, addressing the challenge of verifiable computations of non-linearities in neural networks \[[Xiaofan Zhang' 2021-04-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2104.02251v1)\]. This work demonstrates that ZKPs can be applied to complex machine learning tasks, enhancing privacy and security in AI systems \[[Xiaofan Zhang' 2021-04-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2104.02251v1)\].

Finally, *zkSpeed* builds on these advancements by providing a comprehensive acceleration solution for *HyperPlonk*, achieving geometric mean speedups of 801× over CPU baselines \[[Shahzad Ahmad Butt' 2024-12-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12481v1)\]. This work demonstrates the potential of hardware acceleration to overcome the computational barriers that have hindered the widespread adoption of ZKPs \[[Shahzad Ahmad Butt' 2024-12-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12481v1)\].
