## Related Works

Vision Transformers (ViTs) have gained significant attention for their ability to capture global context in image recognition tasks, surpassing traditional convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in many scenarios \[[17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2105.15075)\]. However, the deployment of ViTs on resource-constrained hardware remains challenging due to their high computational and memory demands \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.08110)\]. A major limitation in existing ViT accelerators is the reliance on CPU for complex operations such as Softmax and LayerNorm, which are sensitive to precision and hinder efficient hardware mapping \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.08110)\]. This communication bottleneck between CPU and accelerator results in suboptimal performance \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.08110)\].

Several works have addressed the efficiency of ViTs through various techniques. HeatViT proposes a hardware-efficient token pruning framework that dynamically identifies and consolidates non-informative tokens, reducing computation by up to 65.3% while maintaining high accuracy \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.08110)\]. Similarly, EViT reorganizes image tokens during inference to reduce redundant computations, achieving a 50% speedup with minimal accuracy loss \[[4](http://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07800)\]. CrossViT introduces a dual-branch transformer that combines small and large patches for stronger feature representation, improving performance over existing models \[[2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14899)\]. 

Token pruning methods such as Evo-ViT and Patch Slimming have also been explored to reduce computational costs. Evo-ViT employs a slow-fast token evolution strategy to maintain spatial structure while reducing computation, achieving over 60% throughput improvement \[[14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2108.01390)\]. Patch Slimming discards useless patches in a top-down manner, reducing FLOPs by over 45% with only a minor accuracy drop \[[12](http://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02852)\]. 

Quantization techniques have been widely studied to reduce the computational burden of ViTs. Auto-ViT-Acc introduces an FPGA-aware quantization framework that improves Top-1 accuracy by 0.47%–1.36% compared to 32-bit floating-point baselines \[[3](http://arxiv.org/abs/2208.05163)\]. Trio-ViT focuses on quantization and acceleration for efficient ViTs, eliminating the problematic Softmax and integrating linear attention, achieving up to 7.3× speedup \[[16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.03882)\]. 

Moreover, hardware-specific optimizations have been proposed to improve ViT acceleration. ME-ViT introduces a single-load policy to minimize memory traffic, achieving up to 17.89× improvement in memory bandwidth \[[9](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09709)\]. ViTA is a configurable accelerator targeting edge devices, achieving nearly 90% hardware utilization efficiency \[[10](http://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09108)\].
