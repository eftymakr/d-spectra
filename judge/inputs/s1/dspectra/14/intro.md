## Related Works

Video super-resolution (VSR) has been a focal point in computer vision, particularly with the advent of deep learning techniques that enable high-quality video reconstruction from low-resolution inputs \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. Early approaches relied heavily on explicit motion compensation and optical flow estimation to align frames and preserve temporal consistency \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. However, these methods often suffered from high computational costs and were sensitive to motion estimation errors \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. Recent advances have shifted towards end-to-end learning frameworks that implicitly handle motion and temporal dependencies \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\].

Deep learning-based VSR methods have significantly improved the quality of upscaled videos by leveraging convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) to model spatial and temporal information \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. For instance, the use of 3D convolutions has enabled more effective exploitation of temporal correlations, although this approach can lead to high computational complexity \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. To address this, researchers have proposed fast spatio-temporal residual networks that maintain performance while reducing computational load \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. These networks employ residual blocks and efficient feature fusion strategies to enhance both speed and accuracy \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\].

Diffusion models have emerged as a promising approach for VSR, offering the ability to generate high-fidelity and temporally consistent results \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. However, the inherent randomness in diffusion processes can introduce artifacts and degrade the quality of generated videos \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. To mitigate these issues, several studies have explored the integration of diffusion models with motion-guided loss functions and temporal alignment techniques \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. For example, motion-guided latent diffusion models have been proposed to ensure content consistency across adjacent frames by optimizing the diffusion process with motion-aware losses \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. These models also incorporate temporal modules in the decoder to improve the continuity of generated details \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\].

In addition to diffusion models, recurrent architectures have been widely adopted for VSR due to their ability to model long-term temporal dependencies \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. Frame-recurrent networks, such as those based on recurrent residual networks (RRNs), have been designed to reduce computational costs while maintaining temporal consistency \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. These models use previously inferred high-resolution estimates to super-resolve subsequent frames, promoting consistency and reducing the need for repeated warping operations \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\]. Similarly, sliding window recurrent networks have been proposed to enable real-time inference on mobile devices by efficiently aggregating temporal information \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c6a8ff4d9dcdf9e5d82310b847af999ed6655f65)\].
