## Related Works

Generative retrieval has emerged as a promising paradigm that replaces traditional search index data structures with a single large-scale neural network, enabling improved efficiency and seamless integration with generative large language models \[[1' 2022-10-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02068v3)\]. This approach leverages a learned differentiable search index to directly generate document identifiers through corpus-specific constrained decoding \[[1' 2022-10-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02068v3)\]. The generalization capabilities of generative retrieval on out-of-distribution corpora have attracted significant attention \[[1' 2022-10-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02068v3)\].

Several studies have explored the limitations and improvements of generative retrieval models. Nonparametric Decoding (Np Decoding) was proposed to enhance generative retrieval models by using nonparametric contextualized vocab embeddings, allowing the model to utilize both parametric and nonparametric space \[[2' 2024-08-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02152v3)\]. This approach improves performance across multiple datasets, including single-hop and multi-hop tasks \[[2' 2024-08-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02152v3)\]. Additionally, the use of few-shot indexing has been introduced to reduce training costs and improve adaptability to dynamic document corpora \[[3' 2024-03-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07220v2)\]. This method prompts an LLM to generate docids for all documents in a corpus, creating a docid bank for efficient retrieval \[[3' 2024-03-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07220v2)\].

The effectiveness of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems has also been studied, with the introduction of Blended RAG, which combines semantic search techniques and hybrid query strategies to improve retrieval accuracy \[[4' 2025-04-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05181v2)\]. This method achieves better results on IR datasets and outperforms fine-tuning performance on generative Q&A datasets \[[4' 2025-04-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05181v2)\]. Furthermore, direct document relevance optimization (DDRO) has been proposed to align token-level docid generation with document-level relevance estimation through pairwise ranking, eliminating the need for explicit reward modeling and reinforcement learning \[[5' 2024-04-11](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08137v3)\]. DDRO achieves significant improvements in MRR@10 metrics across benchmark datasets \[[5' 2024-04-11](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08137v3)\].

In the context of sponsored search engines, ProphetNet-Ads was introduced to address issues with Trie-constrained searching by optimizing the retrieval ability through a looking ahead strategy \[[6' 2023-04-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07466v3)\]. This method improves recall by up to 18.8% compared to traditional Trie-based models \[[6' 2023-04-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07466v3)\].
