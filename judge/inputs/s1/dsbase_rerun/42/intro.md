## Related Works

The growing adoption of cloud-native applications has led to an increased focus on cost optimization strategies, particularly in the context of containerized systems. FinOps, a discipline that merges financial management with cloud operations, has emerged as a critical framework for achieving cost efficiency in cloud environments \[[1' 2024-12-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14753v1)\]. While prior research has explored system-level optimization strategies for resource efficiency and cost reduction in containerized systems, the analysis of network costs in Kubernetes clusters remains underexplored \[[1' 2024-12-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14753v1)\]. This paper contributes to the FinOps field by addressing the financial and operational aspects of managing network costs in cloud-native environments \[[1' 2024-12-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14753v1)\].

Several studies have investigated cost optimization techniques in cloud computing. ABACUS, a FinOps solution, provides a framework for optimizing cloud costs by setting budgets, enforcing them through blocking new deployments, and alerting teams when spending breaches thresholds \[[1' 2024-12-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14753v1)\]. Additionally, ABACUS leverages Infrastructure-as-Code to estimate deployment costs before resources are allocated in the cloud \[[1' 2024-12-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14753v1)\]. These approaches align with the broader goal of achieving holistic cloud spend visibility and cost optimization \[[1' 2024-12-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14753v1)\].

In the realm of microservices deployment, SpotKube has been introduced as a Kubernetes-based solution that employs a genetic algorithm for cost optimization. It dynamically scales clusters using spot pricing and analyzes application characteristics to recommend optimal resource allocations \[[2' 2024-05-20](http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12311v2)\]. This ensures cost-effective deployments while maintaining performance requirements. Evaluations have shown that SpotKube outperforms alternative strategies in terms of cost efficiency and performance \[[2' 2024-05-20](http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12311v2)\].

Network cost optimization in cloud environments has also been addressed through the use of Bayesian Optimization for hyperparameter tuning in neural networks \[[8' 2024-10-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21886v1)\]. This approach leverages Gaussian Process regression and acquisition functions like Upper Confidence Bound (UCB) and Expected Improvement (EI) to identify optimal configurations efficiently. The use of Bayesian Optimization in this context highlights its potential for reducing the number of hyperparameter tuning trials while achieving competitive model performance \[[8' 2024-10-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21886v1)\].

Additionally, the design of switched networks of positive linear systems has been optimized using geometric programming \[[3' 2015-04-10](http://arxiv.org/abs/1504.02532v2)\]. This framework allows for the efficient optimization of coupling elements and node dynamics to maximize stabilization rates and disturbance rejection. The cost of implementing such networks is modeled using posynomial cost functions, enabling a wide range of modeling options \[[3' 2015-04-10](http://arxiv.org/abs/1504.02532v2)\].

In the context of disease awareness in social networks, a convex framework has been proposed to find cost-optimal investments for controlling epidemic outbreaks \[[10' 2013-08-16](http://arxiv.org/abs/1308.3662v1)\]. This approach considers the eigenvalues of a matrix that depends on the network structure and model parameters to derive conditions for controlling the spread of diseases \[[10' 2013-08-16](http://arxiv.org/abs/1308.3662v1)\].
