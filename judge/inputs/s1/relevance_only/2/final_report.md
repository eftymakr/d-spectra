## Related Works

Nonverbal communication plays a crucial role in human interaction, encompassing gestures, facial expressions, and body language that convey intent and emotion \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/1811.09362)\]. Existing large language models (LLMs) often struggle to effectively integrate these nonverbal elements, limiting their ability to create immersive conversational experiences \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/1811.09362)\]. This section reviews relevant works that have explored nonverbal communication, multimodal models, and datasets for nonverbal cues, providing a foundation for understanding the contributions of MARS and VENUS.

Several studies have focused on modeling nonverbal cues in human communication. Similarly, \[[2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16671)\] introduces MimeQA, a dataset of mime videos designed to evaluate AI's ability to understand nonverbal social interactions, revealing that current models struggle with this task \[[2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16671)\].
