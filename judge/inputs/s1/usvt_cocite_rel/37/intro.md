## Related Works

Vision Transformers (ViTs) have emerged as a powerful alternative to convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in computer vision tasks, leveraging the transformer architecture to effectively capture global context \[[4](http://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11929)\]. However, the deployment of ViTs on hardware, especially FPGAs, remains challenging due to the complex arithmetic operations such as Softmax and LayerNorm, which are sensitive to model accuracy and difficult to map onto efficient hardware with low precision \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2208.05163)\]. Prior work has primarily focused on optimizing matrix multiplication operations, leaving these complex operations to be executed on the CPU, resulting in suboptimal performance due to communication overhead between the CPU and accelerator \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2208.05163)\].

Several studies have explored quantization techniques to reduce the computational and memory demands of ViTs. For instance, Auto-ViT-Acc proposes an FPGA-aware automatic acceleration framework with mixed-scheme quantization, achieving a 5.6x improvement in frame rate with minimal accuracy loss \[[1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2208.05163)\]. Similarly, I-ViT introduces an integer-only quantization scheme that enables ViTs to perform inference using only integer arithmetic, achieving comparable or slightly higher accuracy than full-precision models \[[2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2207.01405)\]. FQ-ViT further advances this by introducing the Power-of-Two Factor (PTF) and Log-Int-Softmax (LIS) to reduce performance degradation in fully quantized ViTs \[[6](http://arxiv.org/abs/2111.13824)\]. VAQF presents a fully automatic framework for low-bit ViT acceleration on FPGAs, achieving real-time execution with 8-bit activation quantization \[[7](http://arxiv.org/abs/2201.06618)\].

Token pruning and attention map optimization have also been explored to improve hardware efficiency. HeatViT introduces a hardware-efficient adaptive token pruning framework, achieving significant computation reduction while maintaining high accuracy \[[8](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.08110)\]. Patch Slimming further reduces computational costs by discarding redundant patches in a top-down manner, achieving over 45% FLOPs reduction with minimal accuracy drop \[[9](http://arxiv.org/abs/2106.02852)\]. Chasing Sparsity explores end-to-end sparsity in ViTs, achieving computational cost reduction and almost unimpaired generalization \[[10](http://arxiv.org/abs/2106.04533)\].

Recent efforts have also focused on data-free quantization to address privacy concerns. PSAQ-ViT V2 introduces an adaptive teacher–student strategy to generate data from pretrained ViTs, achieving competitive results without access to real-world data \[[13](http://arxiv.org/abs/2209.05687)\]. Meanwhile, Swin Transformer introduces a hierarchical architecture with shifted windows, enabling efficient self-attention computation and compatibility with a broad range of vision tasks \[[14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14030)\].
