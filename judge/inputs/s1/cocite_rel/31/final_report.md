## Related Works

Recent advances in deep learning have demonstrated the effectiveness of unsupervised pre-training paradigms in natural language processing (NLP) and computer vision \[[25](http://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805)\]. These approaches, such as BERT and its variants, leverage large amounts of unlabeled data to learn rich representations that can be fine-tuned for downstream tasks \[[25](http://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805)\]. Similarly, in the field of genomics, researchers have begun applying these pre-training strategies to DNA sequences \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c43d9cade31600400a0f62beb5bbcc1b548e009e)\]. However, pure sequence pre-training has limitations, as DNA sequences alone may not capture the full regulatory context required for accurate genomic predictions \[[1](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/c43d9cade31600400a0f62beb5bbcc1b548e009e)\]. 

This limitation has led to the exploration of supervised pre-training methods that incorporate genomic profiles, such as chromatin accessibility and histone modifications, to improve model performance \[[28](http://arxiv.org/abs/1301.5948)\]. Supervised training for genomic profile prediction has been shown to provide more informative representations than pure sequence pre-training, as these profiles capture functional regulatory elements that are essential for understanding gene regulation \[[28](http://arxiv.org/abs/1301.5948)\]. 

Several studies have explored the use of deep learning for predicting genomic features, such as gene expression and regulatory elements. For example, Enformer is a deep learning architecture that integrates long-range interactions in the genome to improve gene expression prediction \[[2](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e12e837cb2e9baeaefdcab06fe1c75add8f46389)\]. Similarly, DeepSEA is a deep learning framework that directly learns a regulatory sequence code from large-scale chromatin-profiling data, enabling prediction of chromatin effects of sequence alterations with single-nucleotide sensitivity \[[6](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e018d3883c82c9f85a4c8b7698c3fe52cc40eb39)\]. These models demonstrate the importance of incorporating genomic profiles to enhance predictive accuracy.
