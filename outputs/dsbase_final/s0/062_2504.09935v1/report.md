## Related Works

Generative retrieval has emerged as a promising paradigm that replaces traditional search index data structures with a single large-scale neural network, enabling improved efficiency and seamless integration with generative large language models \[[Kidist Amde Mekonnen' 2025-04-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05181v2)\]. This approach leverages a learned differentiable search index to conduct retrieval by directly generating document identifiers through corpus-specific constrained decoding \[[Kidist Amde Mekonnen' 2025-04-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05181v2)\]. The generalization capabilities of generative retrieval on out-of-distribution corpora have attracted significant attention \[[Kidist Amde Mekonnen' 2025-04-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05181v2)\].

Several recent works have explored the theoretical foundations and practical implications of generative retrieval. For instance, \[[Marwah Alaofi' 2024-04-11](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08137v3)\] introduced the concept of direct document relevance optimization (DDRO), which aligns token-level docid generation with document-level relevance estimation through direct optimization via pairwise ranking. This method outperforms reinforcement learning-based methods, achieving significant improvements in retrieval effectiveness \[[Marwah Alaofi' 2024-04-11](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08137v3)\]. Similarly, \[[Matthias Dorfer' 2017-05-19](http://arxiv.org/abs/1705.06979v2)\] proposed a cost-constrained retrieval optimization framework for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), addressing challenges such as chunk redundancy, non-monotonicity of chunk utility, and retrieval strategy adaptability. The framework, named CARROT, demonstrated up to a 30% improvement over baseline models \[[Matthias Dorfer' 2017-05-19](http://arxiv.org/abs/1705.06979v2)\].

The role of beam search in generative retrieval has also been extensively studied. \[[Mohamed Trabelsi' 2021-02-23](http://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11903v2)\] examined the limitations of beam search in the context of constrained auto-regressive generation, revealing that the usage of marginal distributions may not be an ideal approach. This finding aligns with the broader discussion on the effectiveness of beam search in decoding tasks, as explored in \[[Hyunji Lee' 2022-10-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02068v3)\], which proposed a joint modeling scheme integrating CTC, attention, and transducer decoders to improve performance \[[Hyunji Lee' 2022-10-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02068v3)\]. Additionally, \[[Hansi Zeng' 2024-04-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14600v1)\] introduced a novel decoding algorithm, Language-informed Beam Search (LiBS), which incorporates an off-the-shelf Language Identification (LiD) model to reduce off-target translations in multilingual NMT \[[Hansi Zeng' 2024-04-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14600v1)\].

The theoretical underpinnings of generative retrieval have also been explored. \[[Hanwool Lee' 2025-03-23](http://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18050v1)\] investigated the convergence properties of the Inexact Langevin Algorithm (ILA) in terms of the Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence, establishing stable biased convergence guarantees under specific assumptions \[[Hanwool Lee' 2025-03-23](http://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18050v1)\]. This work is closely related to the analysis of constraint effects in generative retrieval, as discussed in \[[Kidist Amde Mekonnen' 2025-04-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05181v2)\], which derived a lower bound of the error in terms of the KL divergence between the ground-truth and the model-predicted step-wise marginal distributions \[[Kidist Amde Mekonnen' 2025-04-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05181v2)\].

Furthermore, \[[Subhrajyoty Roy' 2023-04-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07466v3)\] proposed a generalized Kullback-Leibler Divergence loss, which addresses limitations of the standard KL loss by breaking its asymmetric optimization property and introducing class-wise global information to reduce bias \[[Subhrajyoty Roy' 2023-04-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07466v3)\]. This approach is relevant to the discussion of constraint effects in generative retrieval, as it provides a framework for improving the accuracy of model predictions under various conditions \[[Kidist Amde Mekonnen' 2025-04-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05181v2)\].
