Recent advances have shifted towards deep learning models, particularly convolutional neural networks (CNNs), which have demonstrated superior performance in handling the complexity of MS lesions. However, these models often require large amounts of training data and face challenges in generalizing across different imaging protocols and scanners \[[Shradha Verma' 2022-11-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.04764v1)\].

To address the issue of class imbalance, which is a critical challenge in MS lesion segmentation due to the rarity of certain lesion types, synthetic data generation has emerged as a promising solution. A self-adaptive network was introduced to handle various MRI sequences and adapt to different imaging protocols, significantly improving segmentation performance \[[Shradha Verma' 2022-11-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.04764v1)\]. Attention mechanisms have also been integrated into deep learning models to improve the segmentation of MS lesions.
