For instance, the use of multilingual BERT (mBERT) and XLM-RoBERTa has shown promise in cross-lingual retrieval tasks, but they often struggle with low-resource languages due to limited training data and linguistic diversity \[[Wayne Xin Zhao' 2022-11-27](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14876v1)\].
