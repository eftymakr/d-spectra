### <a id='category-fine-grained-emotion-classification-frameworks'></a>Fine-Grained Emotion Classification Frameworks: 5 papers found.
In the realm of fine-grained emotion classification, recent studies have explored various methodologies to enhance the accuracy and robustness of emotion detection, particularly in complex and multilingual contexts. Researchers have identified that in-context learning (ICL) faces challenges in fine-grained emotion recognition due to reliance on semantically similar but emotionally inaccurate prototypes and interference from irrelevant categories \[[Zhaochun Ren' 2024-06-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02642v4)\]. To address these issues, the Emotion Context Learning method (E-ICL) was proposed, which utilizes more emotionally accurate prototypes and an exclusionary prediction strategy to improve performance \[[Zhaochun Ren' 2024-06-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02642v4)\]. Additionally, the Sentiment Cross-Lingual Recognition and Logic Framework (SentiXRL) has been introduced to handle multi-lingual and complex sentiment classification by incorporating emotion retrieval enhancement and a self-circulating analysis negotiation mechanism (SANM) \[[Daniel Martens' 2017-03-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/1703.02256v1)\]. This framework has demonstrated superior performance on several standard datasets, including CPED and CH-SIMS, and has also been validated on MELD, Emorynlp, and IEMOCAP \[[Daniel Martens' 2017-03-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/1703.02256v1)\]. Furthermore, efforts have been made to develop lexical-based weakly supervised corpora for low-resource languages, such as Portuguese, where fine-tuning transformer-based models like BERT has shown promising results in capturing fine-grained emotion \[[Yoichi Takenaka' 2025-04-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00013v1)\]. Another approach, SensoryT5, integrates sensory information into the T5 model, enhancing its ability to classify fine-grained emotions by incorporating sensory cues into the attention mechanism \[[Jie Wang' 2024-11-27](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18162v1)\]. These advancements highlight the ongoing efforts to improve emotion recognition through both model design and data augmentation strategies, addressing the limitations of traditional sentiment analysis that focuses only on valence \[[Yoichi Takenaka' 2025-04-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00013v1)\].
