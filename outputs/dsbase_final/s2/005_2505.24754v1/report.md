## Related Works

Instruction-following text embedding is a critical area of research, aiming to generate dynamic text embeddings that adapt to user instructions by highlighting specific attributes of text. This task has attracted significant attention in recent years, with several approaches proposed to enhance the alignment of text embeddings with user instructions. However, existing methods often suffer from significant computational overhead, as they require re-encoding the entire corpus for each new instruction \[[Yuchen Zhuang' 2025-05-27](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21439v1)\]. To address this challenge, we propose GSTransform, a novel instruction-following text embedding framework based on Guided Space Transformation.

Several recent works have explored instruction-following in large language models (LLMs). For instance, \[[Juyeon Heo' 2024-10-18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14516v5)\] investigates the robustness of LLMs against prompt injection attacks, highlighting the importance of understanding how models interpret and follow instructions. Similarly, \[[Hao Zhao' 2024-05-30](http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19874v3)\] presents CRaQAn, an open-source dataset for coreference resolution in question-answering tasks, emphasizing the need for high-quality instruction-following datasets. These studies underscore the challenges in aligning models with user instructions and the importance of robust evaluation methodologies.

In the context of instruction-following, \[[Renze Lou' 2023-03-18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10475v8)\] introduces InBedder, a text embedder that captures characteristics of texts specified by user instructions by treating the instruction as a question and encoding the expected answers. This approach improves instruction-following capabilities by aligning embeddings with user intent. However, InBedder still requires re-encoding the entire corpus for each instruction, which is computationally expensive \[[Renze Lou' 2023-03-18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10475v8)\].

To reduce computational overhead, \[[John Hewitt' 2024-09-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14254v1)\] proposes Attentive Reasoning Queries (ARQs), a structured reasoning approach that improves instruction-following in LLMs by guiding them through systematic reasoning steps. ARQs have shown promising results in various applications, including customer-facing agents. However, they still rely on extensive fine-tuning and may not be suitable for real-time applications \[[John Hewitt' 2024-09-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14254v1)\].

Another related work is \[[Aditi Jha' 2023-11-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13133v1)\], which introduces InF-IR, a large-scale training corpus tailored for enhancing retrieval models in instruction-following information retrieval (IR). InF-IR expands traditional training pairs into expressive <instruction, query, passage> triplets, enabling efficient representation learning for smaller encoder-only models. This work highlights the importance of instruction-aware embedding models for IR tasks \[[Aditi Jha' 2023-11-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13133v1)\].

Additionally, \[[Baolin Peng' 2023-04-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03277v1)\] explores the effectiveness of in-context learning (ICL) for instruction-following in LLMs, showing that ICL can be a viable alternative to instruction fine-tuning in the low data regime. This study provides insights into the relationship between ICL and instruction fine-tuning, suggesting that ICL can be improved by adding high-quality demonstrations \[[Baolin Peng' 2023-04-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03277v1)\].

Finally, \[[Cheng-I Jeff Lai' 2023-09-18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2309.09843v1)\] investigates the effectiveness of small, high-quality instruction datasets for instruction-following in LLMs, demonstrating that subsets of 1k-6k instruction samples are sufficient to achieve good performance on both traditional NLP benchmarks and model-based evaluation. This work highlights the potential of small, curated datasets for instruction-following tasks \[[Cheng-I Jeff Lai' 2023-09-18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2309.09843v1)\].

These studies collectively highlight the challenges and opportunities in instruction-following text embedding, motivating the development of lightweight and efficient frameworks like GSTransform.
