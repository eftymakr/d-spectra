## Related Works

Diffusion transformers (DiTs) have emerged as a powerful architecture for video generation, offering high-quality and coherent video output \[[Tianchen Zhao' 2024-06-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02540v3)\]. However, the large parameter count and computational complexity of DiTs hinder their deployment on resource-constrained devices \[[Shilong Tian' 2024-07-16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11585v2)\]. Quantization has been widely studied for image generation models to reduce storage and improve inference speed \[[Xiuyu Li' 2023-02-08](http://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04304v3)\]. However, existing quantization methods for image diffusion models do not generalize well to video generation tasks due to the unique temporal dependencies and multi-frame processing involved \[[Kai Liu' 2025-05-08](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05530v1)\]. This section reviews relevant works that address these challenges in the context of video diffusion models.

Early quantization methods for diffusion models primarily focused on reducing the bit-width of model parameters without considering the specific requirements of video generation \[[Zhaoyang Liu' 2020-05-14](http://arxiv.org/abs/2005.06803v3)\]. These methods often led to significant performance degradation, particularly in preserving temporal consistency across frames \[[Siwei Ma' 2019-04-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/1904.03567v2)\]. For example, ViDiT-Q introduced a quantization scheme tailored for diffusion transformers but faced challenges in maintaining visual quality during quantization \[[Yen-Fu Ou' 2014-06-08](http://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2018v1)\]. Similarly, Q-Diffusion proposed a post-training quantization method for diffusion models but struggled with the unique characteristics of video generation tasks \[[Wen Wang' 2023-03-30](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17599v3)\].

Recent efforts have focused on improving quantization for video diffusion models by addressing the loss of information during quantization and misalignment between optimization objectives and video-specific requirements \[[Yiran Xu' 2022-06-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2206.10590v1)\]. For instance, QVD introduced a post-training quantization strategy tailored for video diffusion models, addressing the skewness of temporal features and inter-channel disparities \[[Yifei Gao' 2024-07-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.15508v3)\]. This method, called High Temporal Discriminability Quantization (HTDQ), retains the high discriminability of quantized features, providing precise temporal guidance for all video frames \[[Mengzhao Chen' 2024-10-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05265v2)\]. Additionally, Scattered Channel Range Integration (SCRI) was proposed to improve the coverage of quantization levels across individual channels \[[Wenhao Sun' 2024-06-26](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07111v2)\].

From the optimization perspective, several studies have explored the use of attention mechanisms and temporal modeling to enhance quantization for video diffusion models \[[Gyeongman Kim' 2022-12-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2212.02802v2)\]. For example, TaQ-DiT proposed a time-aware quantization method for DiTs, focusing on the impact of reconstruction and varying quantization sensitivities across layers \[[Kwanyong Park' 2019-08-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/1908.07683v1)\]. This approach improved the alignment between quantized weights and activations, leading to better performance in video generation tasks \[[Shuang Li' 2025-02-28](http://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00200v3)\]. Similarly, VRT, a video restoration transformer, demonstrated the importance of modeling long-range temporal dependencies for high-quality video output \[[Shrisudhan Govindarajan' 2022-07-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2207.10357v1)\].

In addition to quantization, researchers have also explored the use of attention mechanisms and transformer architectures to improve the efficiency and performance of video diffusion models \[[Yuanzhe Chen' 2015-02-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/1502.06080v1)\]. For example, the Hourglass Diffusion Transformer (HDiT) was designed to scale linearly with pixel count, enabling high-resolution video generation without the need for complex training techniques \[[Mai Xu' 2017-09-19](http://arxiv.org/abs/1709.06342v4)\]. This approach demonstrated competitive performance with existing models on standard benchmarks \[[Sungnyun Kim' 2024-07-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.03563v3)\].
