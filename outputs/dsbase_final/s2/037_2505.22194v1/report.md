## Related Works

Vision Transformers (ViTs) have emerged as a powerful alternative to convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in computer vision tasks, primarily due to their ability to capture global context through self-attention mechanisms \[[Jacob R. Stevens' 2021-03-16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2103.09301v1)\]. However, the deployment of ViTs on hardware, particularly FPGAs, has been hindered by the complexity of arithmetic operations such as Softmax and LayerNorm, which are sensitive to model accuracy and challenging to implement with low precision \[[Xinyu Shi' 2024-03-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2403.14302v2)\]. Existing approaches often offload these operations to the CPU, leading to suboptimal performance due to communication overhead between the CPU and accelerator \[[Shashank Nag' 2023-02-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09108v1)\].

Several works have explored hardware acceleration for ViTs, focusing on optimizing matrix multiplication operations. For instance, \[[Weixuan Sun' 2022-06-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2206.10552v2)\] introduces a hardware accelerator for vision transformers with row-wise scheduling, decomposing major operations into a single dot product primitive for efficient execution. Similarly, \[[Huy Nguyen' 2025-03-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03213v1)\] presents a framework for accelerating ViTs on FPGAs by exploiting parallelism in matrix multiplication and reducing memory usage through weight sharing. These methods, however, do not address the challenges posed by complex arithmetic operations like Softmax and LayerNorm, which remain critical for model accuracy \[[Maxim Milakov' 2018-05-08](http://arxiv.org/abs/1805.02867v2)\].

Recent efforts have also focused on reducing the computational demands of ViTs through model quantization and efficient hardware design. \[[Yifan Xu' 2021-08-03](http://arxiv.org/abs/2108.01390v5)\] proposes a comprehensive survey of ViT quantization and its hardware acceleration, highlighting the importance of algorithm-hardware co-design. The paper emphasizes that quantization can significantly reduce computational and memory requirements, enabling the creation of hardware specifically optimized for these quantized algorithms \[[Saebom Leem' 2024-02-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04563v1)\]. Additionally, \[[Liunian Harold Li' 2019-02-28](http://arxiv.org/abs/1902.11269v1)\] introduces a self-supervised technique called PatchRot, which trains ViTs to predict rotation angles, enabling the network to extract both global and local features from images. While this work focuses on training efficiency, it indirectly supports the idea that efficient arithmetic operations are crucial for hardware deployment \[[Hanrui Wang' 2020-05-28](http://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14187v1)\].

In the context of hardware implementation, \[[Matteo Bastico' 2023-10-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05572v1)\] presents a novel hardware accelerator for vision transformers with row-wise scheduling, decomposing major operations into a single dot product primitive for efficient execution. This work highlights the importance of optimizing arithmetic operations for hardware deployment, a challenge that remains largely unaddressed in existing ViT accelerators \[[Jyotikrishna Dass' 2022-11-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.05109v1)\].

Moreover, \[[Peiyan Dong' 2022-11-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2211.08110v2)\] explores the use of mixed-precision finite element kernels and assembly, providing theoretical guidance for designing reduced- and mixed-precision algorithms on CPUs and GPUs. This work is relevant to the broader context of hardware efficiency in deep learning models, including ViTs \[[Andrew Shin' 2021-03-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2103.04037v2)\].

In summary, while existing works have made strides in optimizing matrix multiplication operations for ViTs, the challenges posed by complex arithmetic operations such as Softmax and LayerNorm remain largely unaddressed. Our work introduces a novel approach that maps all operations of ViT models onto FPGAs, leveraging a new arithmetic format named Microscaling Integer (MXInt) to achieve both high area efficiency and accuracy \[[Haw-Shiuan Chang' 2023-10-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.14079v1)\].
