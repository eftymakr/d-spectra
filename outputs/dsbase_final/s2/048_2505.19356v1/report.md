## Related Works

Recent advancements in neural retrieval have leveraged transformer-based pre-trained language models to enhance multilingual and cross-lingual retrieval capabilities \[[Aleksandar Petrov' 2023-05-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15425v2)\]. However, the effectiveness of these models for low-resource, morphologically rich languages such as Amharic remains underexplored due to data scarcity and suboptimal tokenization \[[Yanis Labrak' 2024-02-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15010v2)\]. This gap is addressed by introducing Amharic-specific dense retrieval models based on pre-trained Amharic BERT and RoBERTa backbones \[[Antoine Nzeyimana' 2022-03-16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2203.08459v2)\]. Our proposed RoBERTa-Base-Amharic-Embed model (110M parameters) achieves a 17.6% relative improvement in MRR@10 and a 9.86% gain in Recall@10 over the strongest multilingual baseline, Arctic Embed 2.0 (568M parameters) \[[Eylon Gueta' 2023-11-01](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00658v1)\]. We benchmark our proposed models against both sparse and dense retrieval baselines to systematically assess retrieval effectiveness in Amharic \[[Ercong Nie' 2022-12-19](http://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09651v4)\]. Our analysis highlights key challenges in low-resource settings and underscores the importance of language-specific adaptation \[[Hansi Hettiarachchi' 2024-12-20](http://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16365v1)\].
