## Related Works

The study of how feature representations evolve across layers in large language models (LLMs) has gained significant attention in recent years, particularly due to its implications for model interpretability and robustness \[[Md Sifat Hossain' 2025-02-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04355v1)\]. Prior work has primarily focused on analyzing fine-tuned models to identify critical layers associated with specific functions or behaviors \[[Yuxuan Yue' 2024-02-19](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12065v2)\]. However, these approaches often rely on data-dependent analyses, limiting their applicability to post-hoc settings \[[Hongzhan Lin' 2023-12-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2312.05434v1)\]. In contrast, this work introduces a data-oblivious approach to identify intrinsic critical layers in pre-fine-tuned LLMs by analyzing representation dynamics via Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA) \[[Dongping Chen' 2024-07-01](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01505v1)\]. This method enables a more general understanding of layer importance across different tasks and models \[[Zhen Yang' 2023-11-08](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04589v3)\].

Several studies have explored the use of CKA for analyzing representation similarity in neural networks. For instance, \[[Gabriel Adriano de Melo' 2024-08-16](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08995v1)\] used CKA to investigate how representations change during fine-tuning and found that layers with significant shifts in representation space are also most affected during fine-tuning. This aligns with our findings, where we show that such shifts are consistent across tasks for a given model \[[E. Fuselier' 2012-05-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/1205.3255v1)\]. Additionally, \[[Oluwatosin Ogundare' 2023-05-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03715v1)\] demonstrated that CKA can be used to analyze the alignment between pre-trained and fine-tuned models, highlighting the importance of representation dynamics in transfer learning \[[Corinna Cortes' 2012-03-02](http://arxiv.org/abs/1203.0550v3)\].

The application of CKA to domain adaptation and backdoor defense has also been explored. For example, \[[Xudong Lu' 2024-02-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14800v2)\] proposed a method to improve domain adaptation by fine-tuning critical layers identified through representation analysis, which aligns with our findings that fine-tuning critical layers leads to greater loss reduction \[[Wenxuan Wang' 2023-10-02](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.00905v2)\].
