## Related Works

Generative retrieval (GR) has emerged as a promising paradigm in information retrieval (IR), offering a shift from traditional search index data structures to a single large-scale neural network that can integrate with generative large language models \[[Weiwei Sun' 2023-04-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.04171v1)\]. This approach allows for end-to-end optimization of retrieval tasks, but most existing GR models have been primarily evaluated on static document collections, with limited exploration of their performance in dynamic environments where document collections evolve continuously \[[Arian Askari' 2024-08-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02152v3)\]. This section reviews related works that address the challenges of GR in dynamic settings, as well as approaches that enhance the performance of GR models through improved docid design and retrieval strategies.

Early work on GR focused on training-based indexing, where models are fine-tuned to memorize associations between queries and document identifiers (docids) of relevant documents \[[Arian Askari' 2024-08-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02152v3)\]. While this approach has shown promise, it suffers from high training costs and limited adaptability to dynamic document corpora \[[Arian Askari' 2024-08-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02152v3)\]. To address these limitations, recent studies have proposed few-shot indexing-based GR frameworks, which eliminate the need for extensive training by prompting large language models (LLMs) to generate docids for all documents in a corpus \[[Arian Askari' 2024-08-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02152v3)\]. These methods demonstrate superior performance compared to traditional training-based indexing approaches \[[Arian Askari' 2024-08-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02152v3)\].

The design of docids plays a critical role in the performance of GR models, particularly in dynamic environments. Research has shown that text-based docids, which are typically composed of natural language sequences, offer better generalization to unseen documents compared to numeric-based docids \[[Weiwei Sun' 2023-04-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.04171v1)\]. This is attributed to the semantic alignment of text-based docids with the pretrained knowledge of LLMs \[[Weiwei Sun' 2023-04-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.04171v1)\]. In contrast, numeric-based docids, while efficient, tend to overfit to the initial document set, leading to performance degradation in dynamic settings \[[Weiwei Sun' 2023-04-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.04171v1)\]. This phenomenon is exacerbated by the limited ability of numeric-based docids to capture fine-grained semantic information \[[Weiwei Sun' 2023-04-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.04171v1)\].

To address these challenges, several studies have proposed alternative docid designs that balance efficiency and effectiveness. For example, the GenRet method learns to tokenize documents into short discrete representations (docids) via a discrete auto-encoding approach, enabling fully end-to-end modeling of document retrieval tasks \[[Jiangui Chen' 2023-08-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2308.14968v2)\]. The method also introduces a progressive training scheme to capture the autoregressive nature of docids and stabilize training \[[Jiangui Chen' 2023-08-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2308.14968v2)\]. Similarly, the CLEVER model addresses the continual learning problem for GR by introducing Incremental Product Quantization for efficient encoding of new documents and a memory-augmented learning mechanism to preserve knowledge from previously indexed documents \[[Chaeeun Kim' 2023-05-27](http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18952v5)\].

In addition to docid design, the integration of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) has also been explored to improve the accuracy of GR systems \[[Rodrigo Aldana-López' 2022-02-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03012v2)\]. By combining semantic search techniques with hybrid query strategies, the Blended RAG method achieves better retrieval results and sets new benchmarks on IR datasets \[[Rodrigo Aldana-López' 2022-02-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03012v2)\]. These approaches highlight the importance of combining retrieval and generation components to improve the overall performance of GR systems \[[Rodrigo Aldana-López' 2022-02-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03012v2)\].
