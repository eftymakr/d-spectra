## Related Works

Recent advances in sequential recommendation have focused on modeling user behavior through attention mechanisms and state-space models, aiming to capture both short-term preferences and long-term patterns \[[Uzma Mushtaque' 2025-01-02](http://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01242v1)\]. Attention mechanisms have been widely adopted to capture item relationships and user preferences \[[Yuda Wang' 2024-06-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02638v2)\]. For example, \[[Chengkai Liu' 2024-03-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03900v2)\] introduces a self-attention mechanism to model sequential interactions, while \[[Hao Fan' 2024-09-24](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16182v3)\] extends this by incorporating temporal information to better understand user behavior over time. These approaches, however, often fail to account for the heterogeneity of user preferences and the varying lengths of interaction sequences \[[Haohao Qu' 2024-09-02](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.01192v2)\].

State-space models (SSMs) have also gained traction for their ability to efficiently process variable-length sequences with linear complexity \[[Xinyu Du' 2023-04-18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09184v3)\]. \[[Si Chen' 2021-02-13](http://arxiv.org/abs/2102.06950v2)\] proposes a linear recurrent unit (LRU) for sequential recommendation, which offers fast inference and supports incremental learning. Similarly, \[[Pavan Seshadri' 2024-09-11](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.07367v1)\] introduces a state-space model that captures temporal dynamics through state compression, enabling efficient processing of long sequences. These models, while effective, typically lack the flexibility to handle diverse user preferences and interaction patterns \[[Jibang Wu' 2020-01-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00741v1)\].

To address the limitations of single-model approaches, mixture-of-experts (MoE) frameworks have been proposed to adaptively route different behavioral patterns to specialized experts \[[Yang Li' 2021-08-25](http://arxiv.org/abs/2108.11333v1)\]. \[[Pavan Seshadri' 2023-09-20](http://arxiv.org/abs/2309.11623v2)\] introduces a MoE-based model that dynamically assigns user interactions to experts based on their characteristics, improving recommendation accuracy. \[[Jianling Wang' 2021-09-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2109.11059v2)\] further enhances this approach by incorporating a two-level MoE structure to capture both domain-specific and task-specific preferences. These models demonstrate the potential of MoE in handling complex user behaviors but often lack rigorous theoretical analysis of their integration with attention mechanisms \[[Shengyu Zhang' 2021-09-11](http://arxiv.org/abs/2109.05261v1)\].

In addition to attention and state-space models, several works have explored the use of contextual information and negative feedback to improve recommendation quality \[[Yuting Feng' 2022-10-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01942v1)\]. \[[Sung Min Cho' 2020-08-19](http://arxiv.org/abs/2008.08273v2)\] introduces a contrastive learning approach that structures item embeddings based on user feedback, while \[[Isobel Claire Gormley' 2018-06-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08200v1)\] leverages negative feedback to refine recommendations and reduce the number of undesirable items at the top of recommendation lists. These methods highlight the importance of incorporating contextual and feedback-driven signals in sequential recommendation \[[Fan Luo' 2024-07-03](http://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02793v3)\].

Moreover, recent research has focused on the efficiency and scalability of MoE models in recommendation systems \[[Viet-Anh Tran' 2023-04-17](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08158v2)\]. \[[Huy Nguyen' 2025-03-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03213v1)\] proposes a double-wing MoE framework that combines variational sampling with expert routing to improve the accuracy of streaming recommendations. \[[Lanling Xu' 2024-02-28](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18166v1)\] further extends this idea by introducing a hierarchical MoE structure to handle multi-domain and multi-task recommendation scenarios. These approaches demonstrate the adaptability of MoE in complex recommendation settings \[[Xudong Lu' 2024-02-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14800v2)\].

In summary, existing works have made significant contributions to sequential recommendation by integrating attention mechanisms, state-space models, and MoE frameworks. However, there remains a gap in the theoretical understanding of how these components can be unified to better capture both temporal dynamics and diverse user preferences \[[Zhenrui Yue' 2023-10-03](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02367v2)\].
