## Related Works

Representation binarization has emerged as a promising approach to optimize memory usage and computational overhead in recommender systems \[[Haoyu Wang' 2019-06-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01829v1)\]. This technique involves embedding latent features into compact binary sequences, which can significantly reduce the storage and processing requirements for large-scale collaborative filtering tasks. However, existing methods primarily focus on numerical quantization, often neglecting the associated information loss, which can lead to performance degradation \[[Jinbo Song' 2021-02-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2102.03135v1)\]. 

Several studies have explored the use of binarized representations in collaborative filtering. These approaches, however, do not explicitly address the information loss during the binarization process, which is a critical factor in maintaining model performance \[[Lu Hou' 2016-11-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01600v3)\].

In addition to binarization, other methods have focused on improving the efficiency of collaborative filtering through graph-based approaches. \[[Xiaoyu Du' 2019-06-26](http://arxiv.org/abs/1906.11171v1)\] proposed a graph-based collaborative filtering method that leverages the bipartite graph structure of user-item interactions to capture high-order connectivity.
