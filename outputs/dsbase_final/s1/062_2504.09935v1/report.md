## Related Works

Generative retrieval has emerged as a promising paradigm in information retrieval, aiming to replace traditional search index data structures with a single large-scale neural network \[[Hyunji Lee' 2022-10-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02068v3)\]. This approach enables improved efficiency and seamless integration with generative large language models by directly generating document identifiers through corpus-specific constrained decoding \[[Hyunji Lee' 2022-10-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02068v3)\]. The generalization capabilities of generative retrieval on out-of-distribution corpora have attracted significant attention \[[Hyunji Lee' 2022-10-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02068v3)\].

Several works have explored the theoretical foundations and practical implications of generative retrieval. For instance, \[[Arian Askari' 2024-08-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02152v3)\] introduces Nonparametric Decoding (Np Decoding), which enhances generative retrieval models by leveraging nonparametric contextualized vocab embeddings. This approach improves performance across multiple datasets, demonstrating its data- and parameter-efficiency \[[Arian Askari' 2024-08-04](http://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02152v3)\]. Similarly, \[[Kunal Sawarkar' 2024-03-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07220v2)\] proposes the CARROT framework, which addresses key challenges in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) by optimizing chunk retrieval and combining them in an order that maximizes utility while minimizing redundancy \[[Kunal Sawarkar' 2024-03-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07220v2)\].

The role of beam search in generative retrieval has also been extensively studied. \[[Kidist Amde Mekonnen' 2025-04-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05181v2)\] investigates the impact of local and global normalization on text generation, showing that global normalization can sometimes lead to worse performance despite preserving the distribution's integrity \[[Kidist Amde Mekonnen' 2025-04-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05181v2)\]. \[[Marwah Alaofi' 2024-04-11](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08137v3)\] introduces the (G)I-DLE method, which leverages KL divergence minimization to preserve the intrinsic conditional probability distribution of autoregressive language models while excluding undesirable tokens. This approach reduces output variance and improves evaluation scores \[[Marwah Alaofi' 2024-04-11](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08137v3)\].

In the context of constrained decoding, \[[Subhrajyoty Roy' 2023-04-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07466v3)\] presents the PAG approach, which guides autoregressive generation through simultaneous decoding. PAG constructs both set-based and sequential identifiers for documents, leading to significant improvements in retrieval performance and query latency \[[Subhrajyoty Roy' 2023-04-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07466v3)\]. \[[Weizhen Qi' 2020-10-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2010.10789v1)\] discusses the use of reinforcement learning for generative retrieval, highlighting the computational complexity of reward modeling and the need for efficient optimization strategies \[[Weizhen Qi' 2020-10-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2010.10789v1)\].

The theoretical underpinnings of generative retrieval have also been explored. \[[Gustavo Penha' 2023-01-13](http://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05508v1)\] investigates the use of Tsallis KL divergence in reinforcement learning, demonstrating improvements in policy optimization across various tasks \[[Gustavo Penha' 2023-01-13](http://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05508v1)\]. \[[Hansi Zeng' 2024-04-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14600v1)\] introduces the Generalized Kullback-Leibler (GKL) Divergence loss, which addresses limitations of KL loss in scenarios like knowledge distillation and improves robustness in adversarial training \[[Hansi Zeng' 2024-04-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14600v1)\].

In terms of practical applications, \[[Ziting Wang' 2024-11-01](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00744v2)\] presents the Blended RAG method, which combines semantic search techniques with hybrid query strategies to improve retrieval accuracy in RAG systems \[[Ziting Wang' 2024-11-01](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00744v2)\]. \[[Theban Stanley' 2020-09-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2009.13836v1)\] explores the use of multi-task training in retrieval-augmented generation models, showing that incorporating relevance labels during training can improve performance on imbalanced tasks \[[Theban Stanley' 2020-09-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2009.13836v1)\].

These works collectively highlight the challenges and opportunities in generative retrieval, emphasizing the need for efficient decoding strategies, robust optimization methods, and effective constraint handling. The findings from these studies provide a foundation for further research into the generalization capabilities of auto-regressive decoding retrieval paradigms.
