## Related Works

The calibration of predictive models has been a focal point in machine learning, especially in scenarios where accurate absolute values are crucial for downstream tasks. Traditional ranking models primarily focus on the relative order of predictions, often neglecting the importance of absolute accuracy \[[Antoine Wehenkel' 2019-08-14](http://arxiv.org/abs/1908.05164v3)\]. This limitation has prompted the development of calibration techniques that adjust predictions to better reflect their true probabilities \[[Ruslan Vasilev' 2023-03-19](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10761v1)\]. However, existing calibration approaches typically rely on predefined transformation functions, such as piece-wise linear functions, which are order-preserving but exhibit limited expressiveness and flexibility \[[Jarosław Błasiok' 2023-05-30](http://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18764v2)\]. These constraints hinder their effectiveness in complex calibration scenarios \[[Jarosław Błasiok' 2023-09-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12236v1)\].

Several studies have explored the use of neural networks for calibration, aiming to enhance flexibility and modeling power. For instance, \[[Adrien LeCoz' 2024-11-05](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02988v2)\] introduced the use of neural networks to learn monotonic functions, which can preserve the order of predictions while allowing for more expressive transformations. This approach was further extended in \[[Yige Yuan' 2024-11-20](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13343v1)\], where the authors proposed the use of unconstrained monotonic neural networks (UMNNs) to model arbitrary monotonic functions. UMNNs have been shown to provide a more flexible and accurate calibration compared to traditional methods \[[Erhan Bayraktar' 2022-09-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15028v2)\]. Additionally, \[[JunRu Luo' 2021-02-27](http://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00233v2)\] explored the application of neural networks in calibration tasks, emphasizing the importance of maintaining monotonicity to ensure reliable predictions.

Recent work has also focused on improving the calibration of deep learning models by introducing novel loss functions.
