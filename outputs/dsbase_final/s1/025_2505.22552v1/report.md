These approaches aim to address the limitations of traditional methods, which often rely on unstructured text and fail to leverage the structured nature of KGs \[[Shangshang Zheng' 2023-12-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11539v3)\]. The work also highlights the importance of assessing LLMs against curated KGs to identify knowledge blind spots and improve their factual accuracy, as demonstrated through human evaluation studies showing high alignment accuracy \[[Shangshang Zheng' 2023-12-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11539v3)\]. Additionally, while some studies explore the personality traits of LLMs and their real-world behavior, the focus remains on aligning LLM outputs with structured knowledge sources to enhance their reasoning and verification capabilities \[[Yiming Ai' 2024-02-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14679v2)\].
