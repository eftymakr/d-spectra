Additionally, ZK-friendly hash functions like MiMC have been optimized for hardware to enhance ZKP performance, with frameworks like AMAZE achieving 13x speedups over CPU implementations on resource-constrained devices \[[Anees Ahmed' 2024-11-10](http://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06350v1)\].
