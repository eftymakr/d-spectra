## Related Works

Instruction-following text embedding has emerged as a critical area of research, aiming to generate dynamic text embeddings that adapt to user instructions while highlighting specific attributes of text. This task is particularly challenging due to the computational overhead associated with re-encoding entire corpora for each new instruction \[[Yuchen Zhuang' 2025-05-27](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21439v1)\]. Prior work has explored various approaches to address this challenge, including instruction tuning \[[Baolin Peng' 2023-04-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03277v1)\], contrastive fine-tuning \[[Letian Peng' 2024-02-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09642v1)\], and the use of pre-trained models to enhance instruction-following capabilities \[[Renze Lou' 2023-03-18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10475v8)\]. However, these methods often require extensive retraining or are limited in their ability to adapt to new instructions efficiently \[[Juyeon Heo' 2024-10-18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14516v5)\].

One notable approach is the use of instruction tuning, where models are fine-tuned on instruction-following data to improve their ability to follow complex instructions \[[Baolin Peng' 2023-04-06](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03277v1)\]. This method has shown promise in improving zero-shot performance on new tasks \[[Felix Hill' 2020-05-19](http://arxiv.org/abs/2005.09382v1)\]. However, it typically requires a large amount of instruction-following data, which can be costly and time-consuming to generate \[[Aditi Jha' 2023-11-22](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13133v1)\]. In contrast, the proposed GSTransform framework leverages pre-computed embeddings and applies a lightweight transformation mechanism to adapt them in real-time to user instructions, guided by a small amount of instruction-focused labeled data \[[Rob Grzywinski' 2023-11-27](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.16338v1)\].

Another related line of work focuses on improving text embeddings for smaller language models through contrastive fine-tuning \[[Letian Peng' 2024-02-15](http://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09642v1)\]. This approach has demonstrated significant improvements in text embedding quality across various benchmarks \[[Hao Zhao' 2024-05-30](http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19874v3)\]. Additionally, the use of spherical text embeddings has been explored to enhance directional similarity in tasks such as word similarity and document clustering \[[John Hewitt' 2024-09-21](http://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14254v1)\]. These embeddings are learned in a spherical space, which better captures the directional relationships between words and documents \[[Vincent Rossetto' 2013-11-13](http://arxiv.org/abs/1311.3140v2)\].

The importance of instruction-following in large language models has also been highlighted in several studies, emphasizing the need for structured reasoning approaches to improve instruction adherence \[[Mike Lockwood' 2017-08-16](http://arxiv.org/abs/1708.04904v2)\]. For instance, Attentive Reasoning Queries (ARQs) have been proposed to guide LLMs through systematic reasoning steps, achieving a high success rate in complex tasks \[[Xiujun Li' 2023-11-29](http://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17647v2)\]. Furthermore, the use of instruction-aware embeddings has been explored to align retrieval outcomes precisely with user intents \[[Can Xu' 2023-04-24](http://arxiv.org/abs/2304.12244v3)\].

In addition to these methods, the development of lightweight and efficient models has been a key focus in recent research. For example, the LightTS framework has been proposed to compress large ensembles into lightweight models while maintaining competitive accuracy \[[Dimitrios Millas' 2020-09-09](http://arxiv.org/abs/2009.04211v1)\]. Similarly, the MobileSAM model has been designed to be more suitable for mobile applications by reducing the computational requirements of the Segment Anything Model (SAM) \[[Neng Wang' 2023-10-07](http://arxiv.org/abs/2310.04793v2)\].

Overall, while existing methods have made significant strides in improving instruction-following capabilities, they often suffer from high computational overhead or limited adaptability to new instructions. The GSTransform framework addresses these limitations by providing a lightweight and efficient mechanism for real-time adaptation of pre-computed embeddings to user instructions \[[Xiaoyu Hong' 2014-03-20](http://arxiv.org/abs/1403.5188v1)\].
