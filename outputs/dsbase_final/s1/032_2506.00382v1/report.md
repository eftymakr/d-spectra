Such insights have been applied to practical scenarios like efficient domain adaptation, where fine-tuning critical layers results in greater loss reduction compared to non-critical ones \[[Tao Yu' 2025-03-18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14504v2)\]. Additionally, freezing these layers has shown promise in backdoor defense, reducing attack success rates by up to 40% \[[Tao Yu' 2025-03-18](http://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14504v2)\]. These findings highlight the importance of understanding layer-specific dynamics in improving model performance and security across diverse applications.
